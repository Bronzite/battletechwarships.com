[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Retired Texas-class battleship seized from the mothballed Exodus Fleet by Clan Wolverine. Dueled CSR Avalanche and shot down nuclear bomber Angel One over Dehra Dun on Circe on 22 October 2823, then served as saKhan Trish Ebon's early-warning flagship at Gamma 25098 3W on 27 June 2824 before disappearing with the fleeing Wolverines.
