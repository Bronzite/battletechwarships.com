[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

* 3134-11-24 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Skye

Emerged at Skye's zenith point on 24 November 3134 and hammered civilian jump station Gondola Station into unrecognizable scrap under Malvina Hazen's Mongol Doctrine.

• Blood of the Isle, Chapter 23 (24 November 3134) — “Nickel-ferrous masses launched by the Emerald Talon's rail guns snapped off three of the station's six jumpsail supports... Gondola Station was dead.”
* 3134-12-09 // OPERATION LODESTONE: BATTLE OF SKYE [CRIPPLED]
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: Blockading Battleship | SYSTEM: Skye

Forced into a downward evasion by Goran's collision threat, suffering silenced aft Gauss bays, knocked-out portside autocannons, and a hydrogen blowout that reduced thrust by half.

* 3135-09-19 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Graus

Emerged via pirate point eighteen hours above Graus on 19 September 3135 to intimidate Star Colonel Watrous into unopposed landing.

• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 11 (19 September 3135) — “NIGHTLORD-CLASS WARSHIP EMERALD TALON / ORBITING GRAUS / 19 SEPTEMBER 3135... the Emerald Talon's armament gives us no more than the common hostage-taker's advantage”
* 3135-10-29 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: FleetDeployment | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti

Carried Chingis Khan Malvina Hazen to Antares orbit after it declared for her Slip faction during the opening months of the Rending.

• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 15 (29 October 3135) — “NIGHTLORD-CLASS WARSHIP EMERALD TALON / APPROACHING ANTARES ORBIT / 29 OCTOBER 3135... It had declared for Malvina when she assumed the Khanship.”
* 3136-04-03 // BATTLE OF SUDETEN & DESTRUCTION OF HAMMARR'S FALCON'S PERCH [DESTROYED]
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon (Mongol Faction) | ROLE: Flagship Battleship | SYSTEM: Sudeten | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti / Galaxy Commander Malvina Hazen

Crippled CJF Jade Talon in orbital combat before suffering fatal CIC and hangar penetration; piloted deliberately by dying Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti into atmospheric reentry over Hammarr on 4 April 3136.

* 3136-04-03 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Sudeten | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti

Arrived via pirate point alongside Hell's Horses flagship CHH Bucephalus; crippled Khan Jana Pryde's Aegis-class cruiser CJF Jade Talon in broadside combat while taking capital missile hits that breached its hangar deck and CIC, mortally wounding Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti, before hosting Malvina Hazen's fatal BattleMech Trial of Possession against Jana Pryde atop its hull.

• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 29 (3 April 3136) — “Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti reports he has lost most systems in the Emerald Talon... A missile penetrated the combat information center. Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti has suffered severe injury. Ravaged by the Emerald Talon's terrible broadside, the Jade Talon drifted, a dark mass, her main drives cold.”
• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 31 (3 April 3136) — “CHAPTER 31 / NIGHTLORD-CLASS WARSHIP EMERALD TALON / APPROACHING SUDETEN ORBIT / 3 APRIL 3136... Jana Pryde instantly fired back one of her own arm mounted PPCs... on the hull of the WarShip.”
* 3136-04-04 // COMBATLOSS
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: CombatLoss | SYSTEM: Sudeten | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti

Ordered by Malvina Hazen to deliver "Judgment" upon Hammarr; dying Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti sounded Abandon Ship and piloted the 1.2-million-ton hull alone into atmospheric reentry, breaking into three main sections that obliterated the Falcon's Perch, Loremaster Julia Buhalin, and central Hammarr.

• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 34 (4 April 3136) — “The first was Abandon Ship. He owed his surviving men and women, however few, that much. His Khan had not forbidden it.
