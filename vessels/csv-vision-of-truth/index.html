[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Potemkin-class troop cruiser committed fully loaded to ComStar's Operation CASE WHITE assault on Terra on 12-13 March 3068; raked by a traitor broadside from sister ship SLS Swift Justice while Word of Blake sleeper agent Quartermaster Paul Ridge murdered engineer Johan Karlsson, spot-welded the engine-room hatches shut, and detonated an explosive charge on Number Two Reactor's magnetic containment dome.

* 3065-04-19 // ACCIDENT
FACTION: ComStar | ROLE: Accident | SYSTEM: Tukayyid

Survived a severe Number Two Engine Room fire and hatch explosion en route to Tukayyid thanks to damage-control rescues led by Adept Theta IX Johan Karlsson.

• Case White: The Breaking of Chemical Bonds, Pages 6-14 (19 April 3065) — “CSS Vision of Truth, En Route to Tukayyid from the Zenith Jump Point, Free Rasalhague Republic, 19 April 3065... The fire roared back to life with a massive explosion... "That was a mainspace conflagration, my friend. Mainspaces kill ships. We did what we had to do to save Vision."”
* 3068-03-09 // OPERATION CASE WHITE (COMSTAR ASSAULT ON TERRA) [DESTROYED]
FACTION: ComStar | ROLE: Troop Cruiser | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol)

Committed fully loaded to CASE WHITE; fired upon by turncoat sister ship SLS Swift Justice while Blakist sleeper agent Quartermaster Paul Ridge detonated a shaped charge on Number Two Reactor's magnetic containment dome behind welded hatches.
