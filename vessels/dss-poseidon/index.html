[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (2) ]
* 3070 // REFIT
FACTION: Clan Diamond Shark | ROLE: Refit | COMMANDER: Merchant Factor Lorenzo

Converted circa 3070 on saKhan Angus Labov and Merchant Factor Lorenzo's project into a mobile BattleMech factory producing Solitaire and Warhammer IIC OmniMechs with attached Mule housing DropShips and escorted by DSS Tracy.

• Source: 35240 - Masters and Minions (Clan Diamond Shark (p. 154)) — “Tearing out a number of cargo bays and restructuring the internals of the Potemkin-class Poseidon, they were able to create a truly mobile battleMech production site... produces several battleMech designs for the sharks, including the Solitaire and the Warhammer IIC.”
* 3151-04-20 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol)

Arrived at Terra on 20 April 3151 as Spina Khanate's ArcShip CSF Poseidon following the Clan Wolf ilClan victory to dispatch Technician Caste salvage teams.

• Source: 35905 - ilKhans Eyes Only (Pages 20-27) — “Pre-positioned to make the jump in anticipation of a Wolf victory, Spina Khanate's ArcShip, the CSF Poseidon, arrived on 20 April. It dispatched technician caste salvage teams”
