[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (4) ]
* 3070 // REFIT
FACTION: Clan Diamond Shark | ROLE: Refit | COMMANDER: Merchant Factor Lorenzo

Converted circa 3070 on saKhan Angus Labov and Merchant Factor Lorenzo's project into a mobile BattleMech factory producing Solitaire and Warhammer IIC OmniMechs with attached Mule housing DropShips and escorted by DSS Tracy.

• Masters and Minions, Clan Diamond Shark (p. 154) — “Tearing out a number of cargo bays and restructuring the internals of the Potemkin-class Poseidon, they were able to create a truly mobile battleMech production site... produces several battleMech designs for the sharks, including the Solitaire and the Warhammer IIC.”
* 3133-12-10 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | COMMANDER: Khan Mori Hawker

Designated as Clan Sea Fox's roving ilKhanate ArcShip capital under Khan Mori Hawker in the 3133 Touring the Stars strategic survey.

• DarkAge_Touring_the_Stars, Touring the Stars Vol. XXXII (p. 63) — “Fact Sheet: Clan Sea Fox... Capital (City, World): ilKhanate ArcShip Poseidon, no home system... Ruler: Khan Mori Hawker”
* 3145-12-31 // UNITASSIGNMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: UnitAssignment | COMMANDER: saKhan Petr Kalasa

Potemkin-class Spina Khanate flagship ArcShip at 75% strength (83% Omni) under saKhan Petr Kalasa as of 31 December 3145.

• Field Manual 3145, Pages 168-175 (p. 170) — “SPINA KHANATE... Spina ArcShip E/R Poseidon 75% 83% (K) (CO: saKhan Petr Kalasa) (Potemkin)”
* 3151-04-20 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol)

Arrived at Terra on 20 April 3151 as Spina Khanate's ArcShip CSF Poseidon following the Clan Wolf ilClan victory to dispatch Technician Caste salvage teams.

• ilKhan's Eyes Only, Pages 20-27 — “Pre-positioned to make the jump in anticipation of a Wolf victory, Spina Khanate's ArcShip, the CSF Poseidon, arrived on 20 April. It dispatched technician caste salvage teams”
