[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Mobile factory ship converted at Dover V from the Kaga-class carrier DCS Mount Niitaka hull with Clan Diamond Shark factory components, dedicated to DropShip, JumpShip repair, and aerospace manufacturing. On 18 December 3099, while operating in Bikini Atoll's asteroid belt alongside Yamato under the guard of DCS Amber Lotus, Ryū was pursued by four Stars of Rasalhague Dominion aerospace fighters and carrier DropShips, disabled within an hour, and accelerated into the Bikini Atoll star's corona, destroying her entirely.

[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (3) ]
* 3064 // DECOMMISSIONED
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Decommissioned

Completed as Kaga-class carrier DCS Mount Niitaka and placed into shakedown trials, but officially canceled in 3064 after years of chronic malfunctions and a critical drive failure that killed twenty-nine crewmembers including her captain.

• Technical Readout 3067, Inner Sphere WarShips (p. 194) — “Perhaps the greatest example of such a vessel is the Combine's Kaga-class carrier. Though the Mount Niitaka was finished and began its trials, years of constant failures—the worst being the loss of twenty-nine crewmembers, including its captain, due to a critical drive failure—forced the DCA to finally admit defeat. In 3064, the design was officially canceled and Mount Niitaka was slowly dismantled.”
* 3079 // CONVERSION
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Conversion | SYSTEM: Dover

Confirmed operational at Dover V repairing DropShips and JumpShips under escort of DCS Winds of Heaven and DCS Dieron Star.

• Field Report DCMS, Pages 1-10 — “the massive Yamato yard at New Samarkand wasn't dismantled and destroyed in early 3061 as previously announced... the yard was actually dismantled and shipped through various routes to Dover, where it was rebuilt near the system's fifth planet. The Yamato and Mount Niitaka hulls were then refit to new specifications... the massive battleship is now a large mobile factory under the LAW corporate banner... the Ryu is similar to the Yamato, though dedicated to DropShip and aerospace manufacturing.”
