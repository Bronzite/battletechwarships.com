[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Fox-class corvette originally christened LAS Angela Franks during trials between Donegal and Tharkad, renamed LAS Arthur Steiner-Davion by Archon Katrina Steiner-Davion in July 3063, and pressed into service early to ambush Prince Victor Steiner-Davion at Coventry. Destroyed on 9 October 3063 when the Word of Blake Aegis-class heavy cruiser WoBS Immortal Spirit executed an experimental WarShip superjump into Coventry's pirate point to punish the perceived blasphemy of using Arthur Steiner-Davion's name and blew the corvette into small pieces. Because Immortal Spirit hid in Coventry's outer asteroid belt with a blown K-F drive, the Lyran Alliance carried Arthur Steiner-Davion as Missing in Action on its 3067 rolls until Rabid Foxes operatives recovered Immortal Spirit's intact bridge logs following the 16 March 3074 Liberation of Coventry.

* 3063-07-09 // COMMISSIONING
FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: Commissioning

Undergoing shakedown trials between Donegal and Tharkad as LAS Angela Franks with three months remaining before scheduled entry into service when Archon Katrina Steiner-Davion ordered her renamed LAS Arthur Steiner-Davion over spacer superstition objections and rushed to Coventry.

• Patriots and Tyrants, Chapter Twenty-Eight (9 July 3063) — “The Angela Franks. She is set to enter service in three months. "No longer. It will now be known as the Arthur Steiner-Davion, renamed in honor of my poor brother. Press it into service immediately and assign it to safeguard Coventry."”

Prowling Coventry's umbra with three missile-armed Overlord DropShips in ambush for Prince Victor Steiner-Davion's expected fleet, Admiral Haas detected an anomalous over-eight-minute pirate-point IR signature 170 kilometers off his bow and was blown into small pieces by the superjumping Word of Blake cruiser WoBS Immortal Spirit.

* 3074-03-16 // REDISCOVERY
FACTION: Allied Coalition / Rabid Foxes | ROLE: Rediscovery | SYSTEM: Coventry

Following the 16 March 3074 Liberation of Coventry, Rabid Foxes operatives boarded WoBS Immortal Spirit and secured her intact bridge logs, confirming that the Blakist cruiser had destroyed LAS Arthur Steiner-Davion via an experimental 3063 WarShip superjump and resolved her eleven-year Missing in Action mystery.

• Jihad Secrets, Coventry Liberation Aftermath (p. 38) — “Yes, we found the Immortal Spirit. A team of our Rabid Foxes was able to secure the bridge after the fight and we even managed to secure the logs intact. From what we can tell, she was stranded there for—get this—eleven years.”
