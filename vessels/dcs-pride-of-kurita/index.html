Kyushu-class frigate serving as flagship for Coordinator Vincent Kurita's heir, Tai-sho Theodore Kurita. Staged at Deneb Algedi in December 3136 for a New Year's Eve assault jump into Republic-held Altair, the Pride of Kurita struck the Fortress Republic Wall bounce-back and emerged at Dieron's nadir jump point at 0005 hours on 1 January 3137 as a corkscrew-twisted, hydrogen-depleted derelict with Theodore Kurita and its entire crew molecularly fused into the hull.
