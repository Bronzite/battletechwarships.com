CGB Alshain - Clan Ghost Bear / Rasalhague Dominion - - Informal Clan Ghost Bear hull prefix shorthand for Rasalhague Dominion super-battleship RDS Alshain.
[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Leviathan III-class super-battleship laid down at Alshain Naval Yards under Scientist General Gwendolyn (Agassiz) from the late 3130s (introduced 3150). Operational by November 3151 as Star Admiral Tanya Bourjon's Reserve Naval Star flagship alongside Star Commodore Andre Gilmour; asserted Dominion Navy neutrality during the Joiner-Denier uprising on Alshain, stationed Alpha and Beta Stars to watch the Alshain Naval Yards, coordinated six months of fleet supplies via CGB Yggdrasil and CGB Rasalhague, and dispatched rescued Galaxy Commander Lars Magnusson to Rasalhague.

[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (2) ]
* 3150 // CONSTRUCTION
FACTION: Rasalhague Dominion | ROLE: Construction | SYSTEM: Alshain

Constructed at the Alshain Naval Yards under Scientist General Gwendolyn (Agassiz) from the late 3130s as the 2,400,000-ton Leviathan III class (20 DropShip collars, 60 fighters), entering service at the dawn of the ilClan Era.

• XTRO Republic III, Leviathan III Field Testing Summation (pp. 17-18) — “Producer/Site: Alshain Naval Yards Supervising Technician: Scientist General Gwendolyn (Agassiz) Project Start Date: late 3130s? ... Though this vessel has yet to launch, the following is an extrapolation of the monster based on the reports, tentatively dubbed Leviathan III... Introduced: 3150.”
* 3151-11-29 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Rasalhague Dominion | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Alshain | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Tanya Bourjon

Served as Star Admiral Tanya Bourjon's Reserve Naval Star flagship during the November-December 3151 Alshain unrest; declared the Dominion Navy neutral, left Alpha and Beta Stars under Rigg watching Alshain Naval Yards, coordinated six months of fleet logistics with CGB Yggdrasil and Star Commodore Roger Lankenau aboard CGB Rasalhague, and transferred rescued Galaxy Commander Lars Magnusson onto the command JumpShip circuit for Rasalhague.

• Dominions Divided, Alshain Erupts (p. 60) — “Finally, both sides appealed to the Reserve Naval Star, hoping to bring their colossal firepower onside. Instead, Star Admiral Tanya Bourjon asserted the fleet's neutrality before retreating out of easy detection range... Magnusson himself... after two weeks of travel, found himself face to face with Star Admiral Bourjon. The admiral put the Galaxy Commander onto a JumpShip with authorization to use the command circuit to get to Rasalhague.”
• Dominions Divided, The Navy Says No (p. 62) — “Star Admiral Tanya Bourjon [SATB]: I say again. The Dominion Navy will take no part in this debate. The lanes of trade and communication will be kept open... Signal Yggdrasil. We will meet them a day out. Then let Rasalhague know... —Bridge Recording RDS Alshain, 29 November 3151.”
