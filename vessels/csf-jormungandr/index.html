• Field Manual 3145, Pages 168-175 (p. 169) — “FOX KHANATE... Alpha Aimag V/F Jormungandr 80% 42% (FL) (CO: ovKhan Zoie Vewas) (Merchant Carrack)”
* 3151-01-10 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol) | COMMANDER: ovKhan Zoie Vewas

Held station at Terra's nadir jump point under ovKhan Zoie Vewas with Fox Khanate saKhan Ya'el Labov aboard on 10 January 3151, observing Clan Wolf's initial atmospheric drop onto Australia during the Battle of Terra.

• BattleTech-Riptides-Generic, Riptides Part 1 (10 January 3151) — “MERCHANT CARRACK-CLASS TRANSPORT JORMUNGANDR / NADIR JUMP POINT, TERRAN SYSTEM... 10 JANUARY 3151... 'Yes, of course, you know. * 3151-06-17 // PRESENCE
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: Presence | COMMANDER: ovKhan Zoie Vewas

Remained at outer jump point station while saKhan Ya'el Labov detached aboard Mule-class DropShip Jotunn on 17 June 3151 to offer Colonel Luciana Araya Morales and the Illician Lancers a Fox Khanate-bonded Federated Suns mercenary contract.

• BattleTech-Riptides-Generic, Riptides Part 2 (17 June 3151) — “Ya'el had originally considered bringing the Jormungandr down into orbit.
