[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (4) ]
* 2717 // COMMISSIONING
FACTION: Star League Defense Force | ROLE: Commissioning | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol)

Laid down in 2712, commissioned in 2717, served in New Vandenburg (2765) and Liberation of Terra (2777), departed on the Exodus (2784), and was recovered rimward of the Outworlds Alliance in late 3061 to become WBS Blake's Sword.

• Jihad Secrets, Through the Looking Glass (p. 117) — “Name: SLS Zughoffer Weir... Entered Service: 2717... 2784: Joined General Kerensky on Exodus... In late 3061 the Word of Blake brought a heavily damaged and inoperative McKenna-class battleship to the Titan Shipyards... entered WoBM service as the WBS Blake's Sword.”
* 2824-06-27 // TRIAL OF ANNIHILATION AT BARBADOS [WITHDREW]
FACTION: Clan Wolverine | ROLE: Heavy Battleship | NAME IN ACTION: SLS Zughoffer Weir | SYSTEM: Barbados (Gamma 1551 AV)

Entered multiple Coalition firing arcs, savaged CHH Mustang's CIC in a point-blank pass along her keel, and jumped out-system as Cloud Cobra WarShips closed.

* 2824-06-27 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
