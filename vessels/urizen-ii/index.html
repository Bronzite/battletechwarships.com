* 3063-08-15 // CAPTURE
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Capture | SYSTEM: Courchevel

Boarded in an unorthodox EVA assault by 49 Ghost Bear Elementals of Gamma Trinary under Star Captain Jake Kabrinski from JumpShip November Wind (DropShip Burning Paw) and captured intact moments before its engine-room crew could trigger self-destruct, at the cost of twenty-six Elementals.
