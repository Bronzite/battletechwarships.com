* 3144 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: FleetEngagement | SYSTEM: Symington

Attempted to bombard Dominion positions on Symington in 3144 alongside an escorting Carrier until two Nagasawa Pocket WarShip DropShips of Sif Star engaged the corvette and drove it off.

• Source: 35252 - Field Manual 3145 (Pages 161-167) — “the Sif Star successfully drove off Jade Tornado over Symington when that WarShip attempted to bombard the 243rd Battle Cluster.”
• Source: 35133 - Technical Readout 3145 (Nagasawa DropShip (Chunk 14)) — “The Rasalhague Dominion's Sif Star contained two Nagasawas when it engaged Jade Tornado and its escorting Carrier over Symington...
