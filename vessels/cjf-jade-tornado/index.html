Clan Jade Falcon Whirlwind-class light cruiser/destroyer (loosely termed a corvette at Symington in Technical Readout: 3145) that led Khan Jana Pryde's main naval armada outbound from Sudeten on 3 April 3136, engaged Dominion Nagasawas over Symington in 3144, and fought against Task Force Walküre on 1 March 3151, uniting with CJF Turkina's Pride and surviving assault DropShips to break RSS Abundantia's keel and emerge nearly unscathed.

[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (3) ]
* 3136-04-03 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Sudeten

Spearheaded Khan Jana Pryde's grand fleet more than four days out from Sudeten's zenith jump point toward the Clan Wolf Occupation Zone, leaving only CJF Jade Talon in system when Malvina Hazen launched her pirate-point strike.

• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 27 (3 April 3136) — “Khan Jana Pryde's armada, however, led by the destroyer Jade Tornado, was at the wrong end of a four-plus-day transit to the zenith point for its departure to the Clan Wolf occupation zone.”
* 3144 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: FleetEngagement | SYSTEM: Symington

Attempted to bombard Dominion positions on Symington in 3144 alongside an escorting Carrier until two Nagasawa Pocket WarShip DropShips of Sif Star engaged and drove off the Jade Tornado (referred to there as a corvette).

• Field Manual 3145, Pages 161-167 — “the Sif Star successfully drove off Jade Tornado over Symington when that WarShip attempted to bombard the 243rd Battle Cluster.”
• Technical Readout 3145, Nagasawa DropShip — “The Rasalhague Dominion's Sif Star contained two Nagasawas when it engaged Jade Tornado and its escorting Carrier over Symington... Using heavy fire to force the corvette to concentrate on them, the Nagasawas escorted the other Dominion heavy DropShips”
