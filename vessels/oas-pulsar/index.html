Pinto-class corvette constructed above Alpheratz within months of the 2752 repeal of Council Edict 2650; newest of fifteen Alliance WarShips and flagship of Chairman Arthur Ingelmann in 2765. Absent from later Periphery naval inventories; lost or retired during the Succession Wars extinction of Star League-era WarShip fleets, leaving only CPS Redemption (ex-TCS Vendetta) in the 3085 Periphery.
