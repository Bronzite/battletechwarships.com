* 3075 // REFIT
FACTION: Clan Snow Raven | ROLE: Refit | SYSTEM: Ramora

Orbited Ramora during the 3070s after Clan Snow Raven lost two Stars of WarShips by 3070, importing and fabricating Clan components aboard the cruiser so United Outworlders technicians could refit Titan-class DropShips into Titan Monitors on the surface.

• Technical Readout Prototypes, Titan Monitor (p. 202) — “With two Stars of WarShips lost by 3070... most of the parts would be imported or fabricated off-world in the Potemkin-class Wild Swan in orbit. Only installation would occur on the surface.”
