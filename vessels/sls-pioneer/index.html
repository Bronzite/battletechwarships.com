* 2819 // REFUGEEARRIVAL
FACTION: Star League Exploration Brigade Survivors | ROLE: RefugeeArrival | SYSTEM: Paran

Arrived damaged near Paran VII with ex-SLEB refugee survivors, burning out its K-F drive capability shortly after reaching the system.

• Source: 35308 - Interstellar Expeditions (Pages 39-44) — “In 2819, the system's fortunes changed with the sudden arrival of the SLS Pioneer near Paran VII... The SLEB's active defense of Paran against a Directorate raid in 2830 reopened the doors to negotiation... Even as the Vigilant-class WarShip slowly deteriorated... By 2875, the Pioneer was reportedly set adrift in the outer system”
* 2830 // SYSTEMDEFENSE
FACTION: Paran Free System / SLEB Survivors | ROLE: SystemDefense | SYSTEM: Paran

Successfully deterred and defended Paran against a Directorate raid, leading to the 2835 founding of the Paran Free System.
