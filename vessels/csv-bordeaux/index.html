[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Dante-class frigate (formerly ComStar CSS Bordeaux) operated by the Fidelis as RSS Flatus for Damien Redburn's Republic Remnant. Swarmed by Fourth Wolf Guard Assault Cluster DropShips over Dubhe on 9 July 3149, her crew destroyed several Wolf DropShips and fought a bloody boarding defense before detonating scuttling charges along the entire hull, killing the Clan Wolf boarders and buying time for Remnant ground troops to escape.

* 3068-08-01 // EXTRACTIONRAID
FACTION: ComStar | ROLE: ExtractionRaid | SYSTEM: Tukayyid

Pierced the Blakist blockade over Tukayyid on 1 August 3068 to extract Primus Sharilar Mori despite suffering heavy damage.

• Jihad Hot Spots 3070, Pages 18-25 — “(1 August)... The Comstar Warship Bordeaux executes a daring raid on Tukayyid to extract Primus Mori and several key Com Guard officers stranded there by the blakist Warship blockade... "we have crippled the last vestiges of the 'mighty' Comstar navy by gutting the Bordeaux, even as the traitor escaped our vengeance!"”
* 3076 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: ComStar / Com Guard | ROLE: FleetEngagement

Survived the Case White disaster to become one of only two surviving Com Guard combat WarShips alongside SLS Invisible Truth.

• Masters and Minions, ComStar Profile — “The flagships Invisible Truth and Bordeaux represent the Guards’ remaining naval combat force.”
* 3077-10-04 // ORBITALSUPPORT
FACTION: Allied Coalition | ROLE: OrbitalSupport | SYSTEM: Chara

Provided Orbit-to-Surface Fire for General Belle Lee's Coalition liberation of Chara in October 3077.

• Jihad HotSpots Terra (2nd Printing), Pages 55-63 — “The attackers may use Orbit-to-Surface Fire (see pp. 103-104, SO) using the warship CSV Bordeaux (Dante-class).”

Bolstered Damien Redburn's Republic Remnant outside Fortress Republic as the Fidelis WarShip Flatus.

* 3149-07-09 // ORBITAL BATTLE AND SCUTTLING OF RSS FLATUS OVER DUBHE [SCUTTLED]
FACTION: Republic of the Sphere (Republic Remnant / Fidelis) | ROLE: System Patrol Frigate (RSS Flatus) | SYSTEM: Dubhe

Destroyed multiple Fourth Wolf Guard assault DropShips before losing weapons power and self-destructing to wipe out Wolf boarders while allowing her Fidelis crew and Dubhe's garrison to escape.

• Shattered Fortress, The Vanishing Remnant (p. 69) — “On 9 July, when a large Wolf fleet arrived at the zenith point in the Dubhe system, it became clear that the Wolf Empire sought to destroy the Remnant one world at a time. Anticipating a heavier attack from the Wolves, Redburn had ordered the Remnant's sole WarShip, the Fidelis-crewed Flatus, to patrol the system.”

* 3149-07-09 // SCUTTLED
FACTION: Republic of the Sphere (Republic Remnant / Fidelis) | ROLE: Scuttled | SYSTEM: Dubhe

Swarmed by a Binary of Fourth Wolf Guard Assault Cluster DropShips over Dubhe on 9 July 3149, destroying several DropShips before losing weapons power, waging deck-by-deck boarding defense, and detonating full-hull scuttling charges that killed Wolf boarders while Remnant escape pods evacuated.
