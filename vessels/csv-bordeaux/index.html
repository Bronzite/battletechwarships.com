[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (6) ]
* 3068-08-01 // EXTRACTIONRAID
FACTION: ComStar | ROLE: ExtractionRaid | SYSTEM: Tukayyid

Pierced the Blakist blockade over Tukayyid on 1 August 3068 to extract Primus Sharilar Mori despite suffering heavy damage.

• Source: 35026 - Jihad Hot Spots 3070 (Pages 18-25) — “(1 August)... The Comstar Warship Bordeaux executes a daring raid on Tukayyid to extract Primus Mori and several key Com Guard officers stranded there by the blakist Warship blockade... "we have crippled the last vestiges of the 'mighty' Comstar navy by gutting the Bordeaux, even as the traitor escaped our vengeance!"”
* 3076 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: ComStar / Com Guard | ROLE: FleetEngagement

Survived the Case White disaster to become one of only two surviving Com Guard combat WarShips alongside SLS Invisible Truth.

• Source: 35240 - Masters and Minions (ComStar Profile) — “The flagships Invisible Truth and Bordeaux represent the Guards’ remaining naval combat force.”
* 3077-10-04 // ORBITALSUPPORT
FACTION: Allied Coalition | ROLE: OrbitalSupport | SYSTEM: Chara

Provided Orbit-to-Surface Fire for General Belle Lee's Coalition liberation of Chara in October 3077.

• Source: 35305 - Jihad HotSpots Terra (2nd Printing) (Pages 55-63) — “The attackers may use Orbit-to-Surface Fire (see pp. 103-104, SO) using the warship CSV Bordeaux (Dante-class).”
