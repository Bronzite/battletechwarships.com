FSS Lucien Davion FSS-74 Federated Suns / Allied Forces 3066+ - Captured by Prince Victor Steiner-Davion's Allied fleet over New Avalon on 15 November 3066 and recommissioned into the reestablished Federated Suns Navy.
[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Loyalist Avalon-class cruiser captured by Prince Victor Steiner-Davion's Allied fleet over New Avalon on 15 November 3066 and recommissioned as FSS Lucien Davion. Survived into the Dark Age as the Federated Suns' premier capital ship until 19 June 3144, when First Prince Caleb Davion ordered it into low geosynchronous orbit over Palmyra and Draconis Combine surprise attackers overwhelmed and destroyed it with ballistic capital missiles.
