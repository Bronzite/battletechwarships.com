[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Du Shi Wang-class battleship and Clan Mongoose flagship. In orbit over Circe during the 2868 Smoke Jaguar Trial of Absorption of Clan Mongoose, it teamed with Star Colonel Jenna Mercedes's Chippewa IIC fighters to shatter the spine of the twice-larger McKenna-class CSJ Obsidian before Smoke Jaguar destroyer CSJ Storm Cat crippled Quicksilver Mongoose, leaving the battleship drifting out of the Circe system leaking atmosphere. Unaccounted for in subsequent Clan fleet rosters.

* 2868 // COMBATDAMAGE
FACTION: Clan Mongoose | ROLE: CombatDamage | SYSTEM: Circe

Engaged CSJ Obsidian above Circe with twelve bow NPPCs to shatter the Smoke Jaguar battleship's spine, before Lola III-class destroyer CSJ Storm Cat crippled Quicksilver Mongoose and left her drifting out-system leaking atmosphere.

• TRO Golden Century, Pages 54-64 — “The flagships of the two fleets engaged as the Quicksilver Mongoose, a Du Shi Wang-class battleship, challenged the McKenna-class Obsidian, a ship more than twice its size... The two Clans continued to exchange fire until an explosion rocked the massive vessel... The jewel of the Jaguar fleet was disabled.”
• Era Digest Golden Century, Pages 8-10 — “It seemed that the Mongooses were going to carry the day when the Quicksilver Mongoose shattered the spine of the McKenna-class Obsidian. However, the Lola III Storm Cat crippled the Mongoose battleship, leaving it to drift out of the system leaking atmosphere.”
