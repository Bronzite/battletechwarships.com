[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Massive Leviathan II-class battleship over whose construction the 3067 Nova Cat War erupted; survived Operation SCOUR (3078) and Bikini Atoll (3099), continuing alongside Yggdrasil as one of the Rasalhague Dominion's two operational WarShips in 3145 appearing periodically at Alshain. Coordinated Dominion naval operations in late 3151 and was tentatively identified in signals intelligence off Asgard in July 3152.
