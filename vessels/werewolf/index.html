* 3066-08-24 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Clan Wolf (in-Exile) | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Tharkad | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Wolfgang Shaw

Delivered heavy broadsides to rescue Kerensky's Blues and disable the Mjolnir-class LAS Yggdrasil before Phelan Kell extended hegira to the retreating Lyran defenders.

• Endgame, Chapter Eighteen — “Werewolf, Tharkad System, Donegal Province, Lyran Alliance, 24 August 3066. Khan Phelan Kell stood with his feet planted wide against the Werewolf's tiled decking, sensing the shift in gravity as aft thrusters fired to slowly swing the McKenna-class battleship around.”

FACTION: Clan Wolf (in-Exile) | ROLE: Task Force Flagship | SYSTEM: Tharkad | COMMANDER: Khan Phelan Kell / Star Admiral Wolfgang Shaw

Broke the Lyran Fox corvette attacking Kerensky's Blues, detached its Elephant tug to assist the mercenaries, and used rolling broadside fire to batter LAS Yggdrasil dead in space.
