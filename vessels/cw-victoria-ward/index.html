Liberator-class cruiser operated by Clan Wolf's Task Force Mercury on 1 January 3151. Diverted to reinforce CWS Bloody Fang at the Titan Shipyards on 2 January 3151, where a hull-penetrating capital-missile salvo severely damaged the cruiser and incapacitated Star Admiral Dalton Rhyde; subsequently placed in captured Titan drydocks for protracted repairs.

* 3151-01-02 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Clan Wolf | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol) | COMMANDER: Star Admiral Dalton Rhyde

Diverted by Star Admiral Haake Sukhanov to reinforce crippled CWS Bloody Fang at the Titan Shipyards. Absorbed a hull-penetrating capital-missile salvo that severely damaged the ship and left Star Admiral Dalton Rhyde unconscious before Wolf Isegrim escorts destroyed Warwick Station and placed Victoria Ward into Titan drydock for slow repairs.

• ilClan, Page 16 — “A major blow to Wolf morale occurred when a capital-missile salvo penetrated the Victoria Ward's hull... the Wolves used the shipyards' dry-dock facilities to begin repairing damage to the Victoria Ward.”
• Trial of Birthright, Chapter 9 (8 June 3151) — “Star Admiral Dalton Rhyde was a Clan Wolf naval commander whose WarShip, the Victoria Ward, had suffered heavy damage during the fight for Terra. Originally planning to go down with his ship, he was found unconscious and barely breathing when the rescue teams returned after the battle.”
