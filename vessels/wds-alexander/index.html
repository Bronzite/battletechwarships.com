[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Aegis-class flagship of Wolf's Dragoons commander Maeve Wolf. Broke through Blakist invaders on 20 December 3067 to land two battalions of Alpha Regiment on Outreach; after Word of Blake WarShips began nuclear orbital bombardment on 28 December, the crippled flagship's captain launched a sacrificial charge to cover escaping Dragoons.
