SLDF cruiser assigned alongside frigate SLS Alacrity to escort the Sixteenth Division at Newtown Square in January 2588; bombarded the Ard Ri Mountains bunker complex before racing to the Newtown Shoals comet cloud to destroy or cripple seven of Admiral Hakim Wbika's eight Rim Worlds WarShips and recover Wbika's escape pod. Not carried on the 2765 SLDF active fleet command rolls or any post-Exodus fleet inventory; ultimate disposition unrecorded.
