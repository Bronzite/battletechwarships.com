SLDF carrier/battlecruiser heavily damaged in 2765 by debris from the exploding battleship SLS Nebraska during the Periphery Uprising yet surviving into Operation LIBERATION, where Captain Pan Ballard's Swift aerospace squadron shielded her from attacking Rim Worlds Republic fighters until her air wing returned. Unaccounted for following the 2784 dissolution of the Star League Defense Force and Operation EXODUS, and absent from later ComStar, Word of Blake, and Clan fleet inventories.

* 2765 // COLLISIONDAMAGE
FACTION: Star League Defense Force | ROLE: CollisionDamage | SYSTEM: New Vandenburg

Badly damaged by wreckage after a Taurian rebel-compromised fuel cargo ship detonated inside the open bay of nearby battleship SLS Nebraska.

• Star League Sourcebook, Pages 78-83 — “In one instance, a cargo ship laden with highly volatile fuel floated into an open cargo hold of the SLS Nebraska, a Star League battleship. The detonation split the ship in two, and one of the pieces collided with the SLS Cairo, a nearby battlecruiser.”

* 2777 // COMBAT
FACTION: Star League Defense Force | ROLE: Combat

Came under heavy attack by Rim Worlds Republic aerospace fighters while her main air wing was away on a strike mission during Operation LIBERATION; defended by Captain Pan Ballard's light Swift fighter squadron drawing attackers into Cairo's defensive batteries until her air wing returned.

• TRO 3050 Upgrade, Notable Pilots: Captain Pan Ballard (p. 228) — “During Operation Liberation the SLDF carrier Cairo came under heavy attack by Rim Worlds fighters. Most of the warship's fighter complement was elsewhere on a strike mission, and so defense fell to a light squadron headed by Pan Ballard and her Swift... allowing them to harass the Rim Worlds forces, drawing them away from Cairo's weak spots and into its guns' engagement envelope.”
