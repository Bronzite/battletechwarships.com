Flagship of Operation EXODUS mutineers after Admiral Votok and executive officers were killed; destroyed loyalist DropShips including an Achilles-class vessel until Major Elizabeth Hazen led three companies of Ghosts of the Black Watch Marines from NL-12 craft Pride of Odessa on 28-29 September 2785 to breach the hull, seize the bridge, compel unconditional surrender, and host General Aleksandr Kerensky's tribunal in a secondary cargo hold.
