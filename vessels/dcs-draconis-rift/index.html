[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (7) ]
* 3099 // TROOPTRANSPORT
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: TroopTransport

Operated near the Rasalhague Dominion border during the early months of the Second Combine-Dominion War to assist the transport of the Seventeenth Benjamin Regulars and Ryuken-roku.

• Wars of the Republic Era, The Second Combine-Dominion War: Bikini Atoll (p. 32) — “Only Draconis Rift was seen near the Dominion border when assisting the transport of the Seventeenth Benjamin Regulars and Ryuken-roku.”
* 3100-01-15 // RECONNAISSANCE
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Reconnaissance | SYSTEM: Bikini Atoll

Dispatched to Bikini Atoll on 15 January 3100 after contact was lost with DCS Amber Lotus; located Amber Lotus's unsalvageable hulk with all escape pods punctured, recovered Dominion fighter debris, and confirmed that factory ships Yamato and Ryū had been accelerated into the star's corona.

• Wars of the Republic Era, The Second Combine-Dominion War: Bikini Atoll (p. 33) — “With no report from Amber Lotus, the DCMS sent Draconis Rift to investigate on 15 January 3100. What they found was wreckage from the battle. The factory ships had been accelerated into the Bikini Atoll star's corona, destroying them entirely. The wreck of Amber Lotus was unsalvageable and no survivors were found; all of the escape pods had been punctured by weapons fire and exposed to vacuum.”
* 3100-03-18 // ASSAULT ON TRONDHEIM (DCS DRACONIS RIFT INSERTION) [RETREATED]
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Strike Frigate Flagship | SYSTEM: Trondheim

Anchored orbital drop zones over Trondheim until Polar Galaxy's arrival with three Pocket WarShip Stars compelled withdrawal on 30 March.
