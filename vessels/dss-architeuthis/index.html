SLS Marseilles - Star League Defense Force / Clan Diamond Shark - - Original cache ship name before Diamond Shark Watch recovered her at Babylon in 3075 and rechristened her Architeuthis.
[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Mysterious Essex-class destroyer of unknown provenance first observed joining the Diamond Shark fleet above Twycross in 3075; deployed with the 101st Strike Cluster as of October 3085. Absent under its Diamond Shark name from the 3145 Clan Sea Fox Khanate/Aimag fleet roster (Field Manual: 3145), though some older Shark hulls may have been rechristened as CargoShips or ArcShips.
