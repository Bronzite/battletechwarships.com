[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Clan Jade Falcon Aegis-class heavy cruiser commanded by Star Captain Christopher Folkner; carried Star Colonel Damon's Dark Wing Cluster from Apollo into the Dark Nebula in August 3051 to contest Camelot Command against Rhonda Snord's Irregulars before returning to Apollo when Clan bidding law barred orbital bombardment. Listed among the eight Jade Falcon WarShips missing with unknown fate in Field Report: Clans (3079) and absent from Field Manual: 3085.

* 3051-08-01 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Jade Falcon | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Camelot Command (Dark Nebula) | COMMANDER: Star Captain Christopher Folkner

Transported Dark Wing Cluster DropShips from Apollo into the Dark Nebula to contest Camelot Command against Rhonda Snord's Irregulars; barred by Clan law from intervening after Damon lost his Trial of Possession, and jumped back to Apollo to alert the Khans via HPG.

• Jade Falcon Sourcebook, Clan Snord (pp. 34-35) — “Star Colonel Damon's Cluster boarded their DropShips, attached them to the Hawk-Eye, an Aegis Class cruiser, and jumped into the Dark Nebula... The captain of the Hawk-Eye, angered because Clan law forbade him to intervene and blast the bandits off the base, jumped back to Apollo and informed the Khans of Clan Jade Falcon via HPG transmission”
