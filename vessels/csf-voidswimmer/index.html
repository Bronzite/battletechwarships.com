Potemkin-class CargoShip converted circa 3131 at Tukayyid with permanently attached Behemoth-class habitat communities and serving as flagship of Spina Khanate's Delta Aimag. Commanded by ovKhan Petr Kalasa through the 3134 Berenson and Adhafera deployments and the September 3136 Spirit Cat expedition to Marik, forced the Fifth Regulan Hussars to stand down off Atreus in 3139, and remained active under ovKhan Fergus Sennet in 3145.

[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (5) ]
* 3134-06-19 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Berenson | COMMANDER: ovKhan Petr Kalasa

Maintained station at Berenson's zenith jump point three years after a Tukayyid orbital yard refit with permanently docked habitat DropShips including the Behemoth Starmoth while completing hull repairs before jumping toward Augustine.

• Hunters of the Deep, Prologue (19 June 3134) — “CLAN SEA FOX CARGOSHIP VOIDSWIMMER / ZENITH JUMP POINT, BERENSON... within the metal skin of a vessel 1,508 meters long... 'The repairs to the Behemoth proceed apace... cleared to make the jump to the Augustine system within the hour.'”
• Hunters of the Deep, Chapter 1 — “My epiphany occurred almost three years ago during the Voidswimmer's refit at the Tukayyid orbital yards.”
* 3134-09-21 // COLLISIONDAMAGE
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: CollisionDamage | SYSTEM: Adhafera | COMMANDER: Star Commodore Konner

Executed an emergency 2G maximum-thrust burn under Star Commodore Konner and ovKhan Petr Kalasa at Adhafera's nadir jump point to evade an incoming ArcShip emerging within its K-F bubble; survived the emergence shockwave with structural hull vibration breaches and 413 injuries after the docking ring of attached Behemoth habitat Celestial Thirst crumpled.

• Hunters of the Deep, Chapter 27 (21 September 3134) — “CLAN SEA FOX CARGOSHIP VOIDSWIMMER / NADIR JUMP POINT / ADHAFERA... 'Full thrust on my mark.'... as the universe vomited an ArcShip from its belly... Delta Community (the Celestial Thirst, an aging Behemoth-class DropShip attached to the Voidswimmer for long decades) took the brunt of the damage.”
* 3136-09-27 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Marik | COMMANDER: ovKhan Petr Kalasa

Transported Star Colonel Rikkard Nova Cat's Spirit Cat expedition into Marik's zenith jump point on 27 September 3136 and held station overhead during the conquest that laid the foundations of the Clan Protectorate.

• pandorasgambit, Chapter 16 (27 September 3136) — “CLAN SEA FOX CARGOSHIP VOIDSWIMMER / ZENITH JUMP POINT, MARIK... 'You have successfully bargained for and received access to the only assets I am willing to offer at this time. I have delivered you to your target.'”
* 3139 // COMBAT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: Combat | SYSTEM: Atreus

Intercepted the Fifth Regulan Hussars jumping into Atreus to reinforce Regulan garrisons during Jessica Marik's 3139 Operation HOMECOMING, forcing the Regulan DropShips to stand down without reaching the planet.

• Field Manual 3145, Regulan Hussars: Fifth Regulan Hussars (p. 110) — “When the Fifth jumped in to surprise the invaders, the Sea Fox CargoShip Voidswimmer forced the regiment's DropShips to stand down and prevented them from reaching the planet.”
* 3145-12-31 // UNITASSIGNMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: UnitAssignment | COMMANDER: ovKhan Fergus Sennet

Potemkin-class CargoShip flagship of Delta Aimag within Clan Sea Fox's Spina Khanate (70% strength, 66% Omni) under ovKhan Fergus Sennet as of 31 December 3145.

• Field Manual 3145, Pages 168-175 (p. 170) — “SPINA KHANATE... Delta Aimag R/R Voidswimmer 70% 66% (FL) (CO: ovKhan Fergus Sennet) (Potemkin)”
