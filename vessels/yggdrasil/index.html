LCS Yggdrasil - Lyran Commonwealth 3134+ - Operated under the restored Lyran Commonwealth prefix during Operation Lodestone at Skye (3134) and garrison duty at Hesperus II (3145).
[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Only remaining WarShip in House Steiner service as of 3145. Disabled at Tharkad in 3066, briefly taken as isorla by Wolf-in-Exile, mysteriously vanished en route to Alarion after its return, and reemerged in the 3130s to lead Operation Lodestone at Skye before anchoring the defense of Hesperus II.
