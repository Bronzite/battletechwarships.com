[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (7) ]
* 3064 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Free Worlds League | ROLE: CombatAction

Deployed across the Lyran border to Arcadia in an unauthorized operation ordered by Tamarind Marshal Jeremy Brett to rescue survivors of Kristen's Krushers; her aerospace wing secured total orbital superiority over Lyran defenders with minimal losses.

• Technical Readout 3067, Thera (Carrier) (p. 210) — “To date, only one Thera—the Sardis—has seen action, deployed to Arcadia by the 'rogue' Marshal of Tamarind, Jeremy Brett. This cross-border incursion was to rescue the survivors of Kristen's Krushers—Thomas Marik refused to sanction any intervention in the Lyran war in support of his sister. The Thera's aerospace complement allowed the FWLM forces to achieve local aerospace superiority and overwhelm the Lyran opposition with minimal losses.”
• Field Manual Updates, Free Worlds League: Fracture Lines (p. 143) — “It took a rogue operation by Tamarind Marshall Jeremy Brett to rescue the remains of the unit from its pursuers. The de facto possession of Arcadia—though the world later returned to Alliance control—played well to the people of Tamarind...”
* 3067 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Free Worlds League | ROLE: FleetDeployment

Anchored the Tamarind battle group with Eagle escorts FWLS Tristram and FWLS Bedevere prior to the Jihad before passing under Word of Blake control.

• Field Manual Updates, Free Worlds League: Ships of the FWL (p. 144) — “FWLS Tristram and FWLS Bedevere (with Sardis)... Thera-class FWLS Santorini, FWLS Sardis, FWLS Corinth, FWLS Delphi”
* 3068-11-10 // DEFECTION
FACTION: Free Worlds League / Word of Blake Sympathizers | ROLE: Defection | SYSTEM: Tamarind

Abruptly broke orbit at Tamarind on 10 November 3068 with her escorts, firing on a loyalist FWLM cutter before escaping out-system.

• Jihad Hot Spots 3070, Pages 18-25 — “(10 november 3068)Tamarind... the apparent defection of the Sardis battle group. The Thera-class carrier and her escorts... managed to push their way clear of the engagement zone”
* 3076-04-02 // FLEETSIGHTING
FACTION: Resistance / Blakist Markings Reported | ROLE: FleetSighting | SYSTEM: Regulus

Arrived in-system at Regulus in late March / April 3076 on an unannounced diplomatic mission under close-orbit exclusion.

• Jihad Hotspots 3076, Pages 68-74 — “(2 April 3076) Regulus... several news agencies have reported that the fleet is, in fact, the Sardis battlegroup, a force known to have ties to Duchess Alys Rousset-Marik's resistance movement... merchant sailors... claiming that the vessels bore Word of Blake markings.”
* 3077-11-20 // NAVAL BATTLE OF DIERON (20 NOVEMBER 3077) [CAPTURED]
FACTION: Free Worlds League / Word of Blake | ROLE: Assault Carrier (FWLN Sardis) | SYSTEM: Dieron

Disabled and abandoned by her ejecting crew at 07:45 on 20 November 3077; salvaged repairable by Clan Snow Raven on 2 December and renamed CSR Raven's Nest.

* 3077-11-20 // CAPTURED
FACTION: Word of Blake / Clan Snow Raven | ROLE: Captured | SYSTEM: Dieron

Disabled and evacuated over Dieron on 20 November 3077 while operating as FWLN Sardis against Ghost Bear and Snow Raven WarShips; claimed intact as salvage by Star Admiral James Lankenau on 2 December 3077 and rechristened CSR Raven's Nest.

• Jihad Final Reckoning, Captured Ships (p. 129) — “Sardis | Thera | Dieron (3077)* [*Not destroyed; captured by Clan Snow Raven]”
