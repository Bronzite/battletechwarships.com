[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (5) ]
* 3067 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Free Worlds League | ROLE: FleetDeployment

Anchored the Tamarind battle group with Eagle escorts FWLS Tristram and FWLS Bedevere prior to the Jihad before passing under Word of Blake control.

• Source: 10976 - Field Manual Updates (Free Worlds League: Ships of the FWL (p. 144)) — “FWLS Tristram and FWLS Bedevere (with Sardis)... Thera-class FWLS Santorini, FWLS Sardis, FWLS Corinth, FWLS Delphi”
* 3068-11-10 // DEFECTION
FACTION: Free Worlds League / Word of Blake Sympathizers | ROLE: Defection | SYSTEM: Tamarind

Abruptly broke orbit at Tamarind on 10 November 3068 with her escorts, firing on a loyalist FWLM cutter before escaping out-system.

• Source: 35026 - Jihad Hot Spots 3070 (Pages 18-25) — “(10 november 3068)Tamarind... the apparent defection of the Sardis battle group. The Thera-class carrier and her escorts... managed to push their way clear of the engagement zone”
* 3076-04-02 // FLEETSIGHTING
FACTION: Resistance / Blakist Markings Reported | ROLE: FleetSighting | SYSTEM: Regulus

Arrived in-system at Regulus in late March / April 3076 on an unannounced diplomatic mission under close-orbit exclusion.

• Source: 35304 - Jihad Hotspots 3076 (Pages 68-74) — “(2 April 3076) Regulus... several news agencies have reported that the fleet is, in fact, the Sardis battlegroup, a force known to have ties to Duchess Alys Rousset-Marik's resistance movement... merchant sailors... claiming that the vessels bore Word of Blake markings.”
* 3077 // CAPTURED
FACTION: Word of Blake / Clan Snow Raven | ROLE: Captured | SYSTEM: Dieron

Captured from the Word of Blake over Dieron in 3077 by Clan Snow Raven and rechristened CSR Raven's Nest.

• Source: 35307 - Jihad Final Reckoning (Captured Ships (p. 129)) — “Sardis | Thera | Dieron (3077)* [*Not destroyed; captured by Clan Snow Raven]”
• Source: 35905 - ilKhans Eyes Only (Raven Fleet Massing at Terra & Operation Accipiter (pp. 44, 47)) — “CSR Raven's Nest: Thera-class Carrier. Captured from the Word of Blake over Dieron in 3077.”
* 3152-06-20 // HEAVEN FIRE ON LIAO (BOMBARDMENT OF CHANG-AN) [SURVIVED]
FACTION: Clan Snow Raven / Raven Alliance | ROLE: Conqueror Naval Star Carrier | SYSTEM: Liao
