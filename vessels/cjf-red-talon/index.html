[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Aegis-class heavy cruiser of Clan Jade Falcon. Reported disabled and scuttled after the 3074 fighting at Coventry in Allied intelligence, the restored or replacement Aegis-class Red Talon bombarded Timkovichi in August 3142 and served in the 3145 Jade Falcon fleet. During Operation Birthright at Terra's zenith jump point on 21 January 3151, barrages from Gladius Terrae and Glory of the Republic knocked Red Talon's engines offline before CJF Jade Tornado shielded her; claimed as ilClan isorla by ilKhan Alaric Ward on 23 November 3151, she was granted to Clan Snow Raven and rechristened CSR Mariposa by Khan Stephanie McKenna.
