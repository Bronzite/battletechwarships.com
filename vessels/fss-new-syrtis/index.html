* 3079-12-01 // REFIT
FACTION: Federated Suns | ROLE: Refit | SYSTEM: New Syrtis

Engine-crippled and unable to jump in December 3079 while New Syrtis L4 Shipyards prioritized her restoration after Taurian nuclear attacks.

• Objectives Federated Suns, Pages 1-17 — “The only yard capable of repairing the state's remaining WarShips is still months from being able to begin work on the FSS New Syrtis. * 3085 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Federated Suns | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: New Syrtis

Remained stationed guarding Capellan March capital New Syrtis as one of the Federated Suns' four remaining WarShips in 3085.

• Field Manual 3085, AFFS Naval Forces (p. 53) — “The FSS Lucian Davion remains defending New Avalon and the FSS Admiral Michael Saile and FSS New Syrtis continue their stations at Robinson and New Syrtis respectively.”

FACTION: Federated Suns | ROLE: CombatLoss | SYSTEM: New Syrtis

Destroyed defending New Syrtis against overwhelming Capellan invasion forces despite aerospace escort from the 463rd Independent Aerospace Wing.

• TRO 3145 Federated Suns, Pages 44-108 — “The 463rd was assigned as escort for the New Syrtis, the corvette defending the march capital, but they couldn't keep the strong Capellan attack from destroying the WarShip.”
