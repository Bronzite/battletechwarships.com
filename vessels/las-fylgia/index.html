* 3067 // CONSTRUCTION
FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: Construction | SYSTEM: Alarion

Remained uncompleted on the slipways at Alarion's Port Sydney Naval Yards in late 3067 while Bowie Industries and Ioto Galactic resolved sensor and sail-reefing issues seen aboard sister ship LAS Yggdrasil.

• Technical Readout 3067, Mjolnir (Battlecruiser) (p. 214) — “Problems with the sensor suite and the sail-reefing assembly limited the role of the one operational Mjolnir, the LAS Yggdrasil, in the civil war. The second vessel—the Fylgja—remains uncompleted at Alarion.”

FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: Defense Battlecruiser | SYSTEM: Skye

Exhausted ammo stores notwithstanding, drove off attacking Pocket WarShips with heavy naval PPC broadsides on 4 October 3068.
