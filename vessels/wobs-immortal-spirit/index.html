* 2382 // COMMISSIONING
FACTION: Terran Hegemony | ROLE: Commissioning | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol)

Launched in 2382 as THS E. Presley, mothballed 2531, and reactivated 2582 for nearly two centuries of Star League service.

• Dawn of the Jihad, WarShip Profile: Immortal Spirit (p. 117) — “The Aegis-class heavy cruiser started life as the THS E. Presley, launching from the Greenock shipyards over Terra in 2382. Mothballed in 2531, she was reactivated in 2582 and refitted extensively for service in the new Star League Defense Force.”
* 2767-07-01 // LOSS OF THE 568TH FLOTILLA AT EPSILON INDI [CRIPPLED]
FACTION: Star League Defense Force | ROLE: Second Group Flagship | NAME IN ACTION: SLS E. Presley | SYSTEM: Epsilon Indi | COMMANDER: Vice Admiral Mitchell Justin

Led the second task group around Epsilon Indi's far side under sustained Caspar and SDS assault; disabled and presumed destroyed until recovered in 3059 as WoBS Immortal Spirit.

* 2767-07-01 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Star League Defense Force | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Epsilon Indi

Disabled and presumed lost leading the 568th Flotilla's second group against Amaris Caspar drones on 1 July 2767, left drifting in a long elliptical orbit.

• Star League Sourcebook, Admiral Braso's Last Transmission (p. 85) — “We high-geed it to Epsilon Indi in two groups, I in my flagship and Vice Admiral Mitchell Justin in his cruiser, the E. Presley.”
* 3059 // REFIT
FACTION: Word of Blake | ROLE: Refit | SYSTEM: Epsilon Indi

Hulk recovered from elliptical Epsilon Indi orbit circa 3059, refitted, and rechristened WoBS Immortal Spirit.

• Dawn of the Jihad, WarShip Profile: Immortal Spirit (p. 128) — “However, the E. Presley was only disabled, not destroyed, and her lifeless hulk was left adrift in a long elliptical orbit from which the Word of Blake presumably recovered her in 3059. Refitted and re-christened the Immortal Spirit”
