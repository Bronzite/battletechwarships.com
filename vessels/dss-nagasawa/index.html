[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Sovetskii Soyuz-class heavy cruiser serving Clan Diamond Shark at Itabaiana until gutted during the final Jihad naval fighting at Tukayyid. Rebuilt as Clan Sea Fox's Spina Khanate Beta Aimag command CargoShip CSF Talismantia under ovKhan Sha Clarke by 3134 and ovKhan Matthew Horn in 3145-3147, crushing a Regulan naval sortie at Regulus's nadir jump point ahead of Horn's 4 February 3147 planetary landing.

* 3076-05-09 // COMBATDAMAGE
FACTION: Clan Diamond Shark | ROLE: CombatDamage | SYSTEM: Tukayyid

Severely damaged in a KungsArme aerospace fighter strafing run on 9 May 3076 over Tukayyid during Trial tensions.

• Jihad Hotspots 3076, Pages 68-74 — “(9 May 3076) Tukayyid... a strafi ng run by KungsArme fi ghters against the Shark fl agship, the Nagasawa. The Diamond Shark vessel suffered severe damage, and only intervention by Shark merchant caste spokesmen prevented further retaliation.”
* 3085 // UNITASSIGNMENT
FACTION: Clan Diamond Shark | ROLE: UnitAssignment | SYSTEM: Itabaiana

Recorded as permanent station flagship supporting the 17th Air Assault Cluster at Itabaiana as of 3085 prior to its reconstruction as CSF Talismantia.

• Field Manual 3085, Clan Diamond Shark TO&E (3085) — “17th Air Assault Cluster V/F CDS Nagasawa”
* 3134-07-28 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Remulac | COMMANDER: ovKhan Sha Clarke

Following her gutting at Tukayyid during the Jihad, the 384-year-old cruiser was reconstructed as Spina Khanate Beta Aimag's 800-meter command CargoShip CSF Talismantia; recharged her solar sail at Remulac's zenith jump point on 28 July 3134 prior to jumping to Savannah.

• Hunters of the Deep, Chapter 8 (28 July 3134) — “CLAN SEA FOX CARGOSHIP TALISMANTIA / ZENITH JUMP POINT, REMULAC... OvKhan Sha Clarke stood on the observation deck and gazed back along the eight-hundred-meter length of his ship: the command CargoShip for Beta Aimag of the Spina Khanate... In another life, another age, this vessel bore the name Nagasawa. During the dark night of the Jihad, it was gutted at the final naval battle of Tukayyid. With the molting of the Diamond Sharks to their original name and a new form, so this vessel gained a new lease on life: transformed into a CargoShip”
* 3145-12-31 // UNITASSIGNMENT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: UnitAssignment | COMMANDER: ovKhan Matthew Horn

Sovetskii Soyuz-class Heavy Cruiser flagship of Beta Aimag within Clan Sea Fox's Spina Khanate (80% strength, 31% Omni) as of 31 December 3145.

• Field Manual 3145, Pages 168-175 (p. 170) — “SPINA KHANATE... Beta Aimag V/R Talismantia 80% 31% (FL) (CO: ovKhan Matthew Horn) (Sovetskii Soyuz)”
* 3147 // COMBAT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: Combat | SYSTEM: Regulus | COMMANDER: ovKhan Matthew Horn

Anchored Spina Khanate's blockade of Regulus in early 3147. When a Regulan naval strike force attacked CSF Talismantia at Regulus's nadir jump point in a failed bid to break the blockade, the attackers were wiped out with all hands lost, prompting ovKhan Matthew Horn to deploy Beta Aimag ground forces outside Regulus City on 4 February 3147.

• Hunting Season, Chapter 13 (4 February 3147) — “Here on Regulus, though, their naval strike had targeted the CSF Talismantia and failed so badly that now the Sea Foxes were sending groundpounders planetside... 'there was a naval battle at the nadir jump point—one of our strike forces against the damn Sea Foxes. But all of our hands were lost...'”
