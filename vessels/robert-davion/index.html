* 3062-10-08 // CONSTRUCTION
FACTION: Federated Commonwealth / Lyran Alliance | ROLE: Construction | SYSTEM: Kathil | COMMANDER: Rear Admiral Jonathan Kerr (XO)

Completing final fitting-out at McKenna Shipyards gantry over Kathil two months ahead of her commissioning ceremony and tragic 7 December 3062 loss.

• Flashpoint, Chapter One (8 October 3062) — “The Robert Davion hung in its construction gantry against a starscape of black sackcloth... Admiral Jonathan Kerr... Executive officer... Two months before the official ceremony and my investiture aboard.”
* 3062-12-07 // COMBATLOSS
FACTION: Federated Commonwealth / Loyalist Command | ROLE: CombatLoss | SYSTEM: Kathil | COMMANDER: Admiral Jonathan Kerr

Commandeered from the McKenna Shipyards by Admiral Jonathan Kerr to support Hauptmann General Mitchell Weintraub's Eighth FedCom RCT. While attempting to engage Wolf's Dragoons' incoming transports on 7 December 3062, the cruiser was rammed by a disabled DropShip and plunged into Kathil's atmosphere, breaking apart on re-entry.

• FedCom Civil War, Flashpoint: Kathil (p. 27) — “Weintraub also gained the support of Admiral Jonathan Kerr, a Lyran national who had commandeered the recently-completed Avalon-class battlecruiser FCS Robert Davion from the McKenna Shipyards... Worse for all, the Robert Davion, while attempting to engage the Dragoons' incoming transports, was struck by a disabled DropShip and fell into Kathil's gravity well, breaking up in the atmosphere.”
