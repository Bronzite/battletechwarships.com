Returned to service in 3062 after an overhaul correcting early technical problems discovered following her initial 3058 deployment.

• Technical Readout 3067, Feng Huang (Cruiser) (p. 212) — “The first Feng Huang (which translates as Phoenix, to coincide with Sun-Tzu's Xin Sheng) class of cruiser—the Elias Jung—was originally deployed in 3058. FACTION: Capellan Confederation | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Milos

Intercepted the Eridani Light Horse SLDF peacekeeping flotilla arriving at Milos, forcing them to fall back to Kittery before later slipping in-system once the cruiser departed.
