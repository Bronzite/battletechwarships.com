* 3059-05-13 // DEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Deployment | SYSTEM: Transfer Facility 4

• Source: E-CAT36093_SHRAPNEL The Official BattleTech Magazine Issue 04-4f44fa58d50e1928c44b51261dd03cd093c19558 (Ghost Ships Galore: The Pride of New Samarkand (pp. 101-102)) — “The vessel embarked under these orders on 13 May 3059, coinciding with the launch of Operation Bird Dog.
