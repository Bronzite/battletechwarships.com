Entered DCMS service in November 3058 as the third Kyushu-class frigate alongside sister ship DCS Galedon Explorer.

• Shrapnel, Issue #4, Ghost Ships Galore: The Pride of New Samarkand (p. 100) — “Today, we look into the mysterious fate of the DCS Pride of New Samarkand. The third Kyushu-class frigate, it entered service in November 3058 alongside its sibling ship the Galedon Explorer.”
* 3059-05-13 // DEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: Deployment | SYSTEM: Transfer Facility 4

Embarked on 13 May 3059 under strict communications blackout to raid Smoke Jaguar supply depot Transfer Facility 4 in support of Operation Bulldog and Task Force Serpent, vanishing along a trail of marker buoys just short of the facility.
