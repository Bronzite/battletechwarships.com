[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Flagship of SLDF Eighth Fleet Commander Admiral Vicho Nnamani (homeported at Tharkad from 2755). During the July 2772 Battle for Lone Star, while leading a combined Eighth and Thirteenth Fleet task force against Lone Star's reprogrammed Space Defense System near its moon Vespa, Caspar drone WarShips singled out SLS Quéribus early in the final station assault, destroying the flagship and killing Admiral Nnamani.
