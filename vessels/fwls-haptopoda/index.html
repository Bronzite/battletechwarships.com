[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Zechetinu II-class corvette repaired by the Principality of Regulus following the capture of Clipperton and deployed alongside the Fourth Regulan Hussars in Task Force Executioner to deliver orbital fire support over Zone Epsilon-3 on Gibson on 23 January 3079. Absent from Field Manual: 3145, which records only Menelaus, Lancelot, and Delos remaining in the Free Worlds League Navy.
