* 3063-09-13 // SHOWOFFORCE
FACTION: Federated Commonwealth / Allied Forces | ROLE: ShowOfForce | SYSTEM: New Capetown

Rendezvoused with Prince Victor Steiner-Davion over New Capetown after six months AWOL from Kittery, underscoring his address at the Royal New Capetown Military Academy.

• Patriots and Tyrants, Chapter Twenty-Nine (13 September 3063) — “the Avalon-class Melissa Davion had spent the better part of six months finding Victor after going AWOL from its post over Kittery. Victor was not above using them as a trump card.”

FACTION: Allied Forces (Victor Steiner-Davion) | ROLE: Orbital Covering Flagship | SYSTEM: Tikonov | COMMANDER: Vice Admiral Kristoffer Hartford

Intercepted the LAS Katrina Steiner as it moved to bombard the planetary surface, driving the Loyalist corvette out of orbital engagement range.

Drove off Loyalist Fox-class corvette LAS Katrina Steiner on 21 February 3065 to clear Victor Steiner-Davion's outbound withdrawal from Tikonov, shielded by an Overlord-A3 assault DropShip that sacrificed itself protecting the cruiser.

* 3066-09-07 // SYSTEMPATROL
FACTION: Federated Suns | ROLE: SystemPatrol | SYSTEM: Tikonov

Conducted orbital search patrols over Tikonov on 7 September 3066 while Victor Steiner-Davion planned the assault on New Avalon.

• Endgame, Chapter Nineteen — “7 September 3066... The Melissa Davion was already running a search pattern, the WarShip working to ferret out the Capellans.”

FACTION: Allied Forces (Victor Steiner-Davion) | ROLE: Allied Fleet Flagship | SYSTEM: New Avalon | COMMANDER: Vice Admiral Kristoffer Hartford

Led the arrowhead formation through intense naval Gauss and laser duels, maneuvering into the aft quarter of the Lucien Davion to compel its unconditional surrender.

* 3066-11-15 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Federated Commonwealth / Allied Forces | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: New Avalon

Flagship of Prince Victor Steiner-Davion during the 15 November 3066 orbital victory over Katherine's Loyalist cruisers above New Avalon before later falling into Word of Blake hands.

• FedCom Civil War, The Battle for New Avalon (pp. 170-171) — “Victor arrived in-system on 8 November at the head of a virtual armada, including the FCS Melissa Davion and four Fox-class corvettes.”
* 3078-08-15 // OPERATION SCOUR: COALITION NAVAL ASSAULT ON TERRA & TITAN YARDS [SURVIVED]
FACTION: Word of Blake | ROLE: Terran Defense Cruiser | NAME IN ACTION: WBS Beneficence (ex-FCS Melissa Davion) | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol)

Captured Avalon-class cruiser fighting under Word of Blake control over Terra on 17 August 3078; lured Vice Admiral Walker Clay's FSS Indomitable and Rear Admiral Marcelle's FSS Admiral Michael Saille out of Task Force Earthbound's anti-missile screen, destroying Indomitable and crippling Saille before escaping Sol prior to Terra's fall (later destroyed at Circinus in 3081).
