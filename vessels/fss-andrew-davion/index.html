[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Avalon-class heavy cruiser seized while still incomplete at Kathil's McKenna Shipyards in early 3065 by a Katherine Loyalist remnant of the Second Chisholm's Raiders and jumped out-system; unlike sister cruiser FCS Hanse Davion, it never rejoined Loyalist forces and was officially carried on the September 3067 AFFS rolls as missing with unknown whereabouts, never reappearing through 3152.

[ OPERATIONAL SERVICE RECORD & ENGAGEMENT CHRONOLOGY (2) ]
* 3064-07-30 // CONSTRUCTION
FACTION: Armed Forces of the Federated Suns (Allied) | ROLE: Construction | SYSTEM: Kathil

Present at the McKenna Shipyards alongside sister cruiser FCS Hanse Davion—both still far from completion—during Marshal Nathaniel Hasek's orbital blockade against Hauptmann General Helen Eisner's Second Chisholm's Raiders assault squadron.

• FedCom Civil War, Wave Three: Kathil (p. 95) — “Even the presence of the FCS Andrew Davian and the FCS Hanse Davian, both Avalon-class cruisers (albeit still far from completion), wasn't enough to deter the suicidal assault.”
* 3065 // CAPTURED
FACTION: Second Chisholm's Raiders (Katherine Loyalist Remnant) | ROLE: Captured | SYSTEM: Kathil

Seized at the McKenna Shipyards as fighting on Kathil ended in early 3065 alongside sister cruiser FCS Hanse Davion by a Loyalist remnant of the Second Chisholm's Raiders and immediately jumped out of the Kathil system.

• Imminent Crisis, Chapter Four (22 July 3065) — “As you know, the fighting on Kathil ended earlier this year, with the shipyards and the world now firmly in our hands. What almost no one knows, and we ourselves have only recently learned, is that a remnant of the Second Chisholm's Raiders was able to secure the McKenna Shipyards long enough to take control of both the FCS Andrew Davion and the FCS Hanse Davion. They immediately jumped out of the system and have not been seen since.”
