[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Veteran Aegis-class cruiser that fought as NCS Blade in Operation SCOUR (3078) and later served the Republic of the Sphere. Crippled by SLS McKenna's Pride on 1 January 3151 during Operation Nostos, she steered into a suicidal ramming run against Kerensky's flagship only to have Wolf assault DropShip CWS White Lupu intercept and shatter her bow.
