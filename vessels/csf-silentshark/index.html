Merchant Carrack-variant CargoShip serving as Alpha Aimag flagship within Clan Sea Fox's Spina Khanate under ovKhan Ike Hawker at the end of 3145 and Star Commodore Marika Sutherland in 3147. On 31 January 3147, she survived a Regulan nuclear capital-missile ambush at Harmony's zenith jump point with damaged maneuvering thrusters and hull craters while Hammerhead Star and NL-45 gunboat Stalker of the Deep destroyed the attackers.

* 3147-01-31 // COMBAT
FACTION: Clan Sea Fox | ROLE: Combat | SYSTEM: Harmony | COMMANDER: Star Commodore Marika Sutherland

Anchored Spina Khanate's blockade at Harmony's zenith jump point alongside Hammerhead Star's Nagasawa DropShips and attached NL-45 gunboat Stalker of the Deep. Took multiple nuclear capital-missile impacts from roughly two dozen attacking Regulan Leopard and Union DropShips, losing maneuvering thrusters and suffering deep flank craters while her escorts destroyed the entire strike force, leaving repairs estimated at several weeks.

• Hunting Season, Chapter 13 (31 January 3147) — “At the core of the blockade lay the CSF Silentshark, a massive Carrack-class CargoShip... 'This is Star Commodore Marika Sutherland to all Hammerhead Star vessels... Protect the Silentshark at all costs'... 'Silentshark reports her maneuvering thrusters are damaged.'... 'Wounded but still alive, sir. Repairs are underway, but are estimated at a matter of weeks.'”
• Technical Readout 3150, NL-45 Gunboat Notable Units: Stalker of the Deep (p. 214) — “Assigned to Alpha Aimag and stationed aboard CSF Silentshark, Stalker was providing overwatch for the inspection of a JumpShip when the Regulans launched a surprise attack... Clan Sea Fox emerged victorious, but CSF Silentshark suffered damage to its maneuvering thrusters. Stuck at the zenith jump point, it is up to Stalker of the Deep and her sister ships to continue the blockade and protect Silentshark from any further attacks.”
