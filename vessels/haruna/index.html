Kyushu-class frigate contributed by Coordinator Theodore Kurita to Task Force Serpent under Tai-sa Hideki. Fought in the 15 December 3059 Deep Periphery Battle of Trafalgar against Clan Ghost Bear, embarked the captured Clan DropShip Stiletto for Major Ryan's Operation Stalking Tiger DEST insertion onto Huntress in February-March 3060, and is unlisted on the 3067 Field Manual: Updates Draconis Combine naval inventory following Task Force Serpent.

* 3059-12-15 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: FleetEngagement

Fought alongside flagship ISS Invisible Truth during Task Force Serpent's 15 December 3059 Battle of Trafalgar against Clan Ghost Bear WarShips, bracketing CGB Shining Claw and embarking the captured Clan DropShip Stiletto on 16 December during post-battle repairs.

• The Hunters, Chapter Twenty-Four — “The Kyushu Class frigate Haruna, the Draconis Combine's contribution to Task Force Serpent, maneuvered alongside the Invisible Truth to bracket the Shining Claw.”
• The Hunters, Chapter Twenty-Eight (16 December 3059) — “'Stiletto has been assigned to the Haruna. For now, we're going to leave the OmniMechs where they are, if that's all right with you, Marshal Bryan.'”
* 3060-01-03 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: FleetDeployment

Assigned on 30 December 3058/3 January 3060 in Task Force Serpent staff briefings aboard ISS Invisible Truth to detach ahead of the main armada for Operation Stalking Tiger, using Agent Trent's Smoke Jaguar recognition codes and captured DropShip Stiletto to insert Major Ryan's DEST special forces ahead of the Huntress assault.

• The Hunters, Chapter Twenty-Nine — “'What I propose is this: detach the Haruna for this mission. We'll go in via a pirate point while the rest of the fleet emerges at the jump points. We'll make a fast in-run, hit the control center, disable it, and withdraw into the mountains above Lootera.'”
• The Hunters, Chapter Thirty-One (03 January 3060) — “The Haruna will jump in and out at the zenith point... We'll be using recognition codes supplied by Agent Trent and masquerading as Clan ships... Since we captured a Clan DropShip, we may as well use it. The Stiletto will detach... The Haruna will go through a standard recharge and jump outsystem again.”
* 3060-02-19 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Draconis Combine | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Huntress

Masqueraded under Smoke Jaguar recognition codes as CSJ Queen Lynx to deploy DEST commandos aboard Stiletto into the Huntress system ahead of Task Force Serpent before recharging and jumping outsystem.

• Sword and Fire, Chapter Eleven (19 February 3060) — “The Haruna is a Kyushu Class frigate with a mass of about six hundred twenty-five thousand tons. The ship we're masquerading as, the Queen Lynx I think she's called, masses only about five hundred ninety-five thousand tons.”
• Sword and Fire, BattleTech Legends: Sword and Fire — “The Haruna had jumped outsystem. The DEST troopers were now truly on their own.”
* 3060-03-19 // BATTLE OF HUNTRESS (ZENITH JUMP POINT) [SURVIVED]
FACTION: Second Star League / Draconis Combine | ROLE: Second Section Lead Frigate | SYSTEM: Huntress | COMMANDER: Tai-sa Hideki

Led Commodore Beresick's second flanking section alongside FCS Antrim and SLS Ranger to attack Smoke Jaguar JumpShips while targeted for capture by CSJ Ripper.
