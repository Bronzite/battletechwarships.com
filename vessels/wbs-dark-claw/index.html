[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Essex (2750)-class destroyer originally deployed by Clan Smoke Jaguar to escort Tau ('Huntress') Galaxy at Wayside V (Wildcat) in 3058. Damaged by Stirling's Fusiliers DropShip Bull Run (5 July 3058) and disabled while destroying the Nova Cat Mystic Naval Star flagship at Wayside V (8 August 3058), later recovered into Word of Blake service as WBS Dark Claw and destroyed during the August 3071 48th Shadow Division assault on Necromo (logged under 3072 in postwar Republic summary tables).

* 3058-07-05 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Clan Smoke Jaguar | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Wayside V (Wildcat) | COMMANDER: Star Captain Klark Chrisholm

Ambushed while intercepting Northwind Highlanders DropShip Bull Run over Wayside V when explosive-rigged escape pods and lifeboats detonated against its hull, knocking out long-range sensors, breaching Alpha deck, and forcing emergency coolant bypasses.

• Impetus of War, Chapter Twenty-One (5 July 3058) — “Star Captain Klark Chrisholm, commander of the Dark Claw... "We have lost long-range sensors. Our external array took a direct hit. Starboard was hit by three of their life boats and has sustained moderate damage, with a hull breach of Alpha deck."”
* 3058-08-08 // BATTLE OF WAYSIDE V (WILDCAT) [DISABLED]
FACTION: Clan Smoke Jaguar | ROLE: Tau Galaxy Escort Destroyer | NAME IN ACTION: CSJ Dark Claw | SYSTEM: Wayside V (Wildcat) | COMMANDER: Star Captain Klark Chrisholm

Destroyed the Nova Cat Mystic Naval Star command WarShip in orbital combat before being disabled and reported destroyed on the ground; later salvaged and operated by the Word of Blake until 3072.

* 3058-08-08 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: Clan Smoke Jaguar | ROLE: FleetEngagement | SYSTEM: Wayside V (Wildcat) | COMMANDER: Star Captain Klark Chrisholm

Engaged invading Clan Nova Cat WarShips over Wayside V, destroying the Nova Cat Mystic Naval Star command vessel before being disabled (and reported destroyed by Star Colonel Santin West), later surviving to be operated by the Word of Blake as WBS Dark Claw.

• Field Manual ComStar, Clan Nova Cat Naval Assets (p. 110) — “The Mystic Naval Star command vessel was destroyed while battling the Smoke Jaguar Essex-class Dark Claw WarShip at Wayside V on 8 August 3058.”
• Impetus of War, Chapter Forty-Eight (8 August 3058) — “"Our WarShips have battled too, and despite losses, we have destroyed their vessel."”
* 3071-08-01 // COMBATLOSS
FACTION: Captured / Word of Blake | ROLE: CombatLoss | SYSTEM: Necromo

Destroyed fighting Capellan shipyard defenses over Necromo during the August 3071 Forty-eighth Shadow Division assault (later catalogued under 3072 in postwar Republic tables after months of Confederation HPG blackout).

• Jihad Secrets, 48th Shadow Division (p. 106) — “aided in the destruction of the Capellan Warships Sundermann Rhys and Anhui (though the Word may have lost a Warship in the bargain).”
• Necromo Nightmare, Gamemaster's Sourcebook: Necromo (p. 19) — “because of the loss of a Blakist WarShip to the Capellans' naval defense.”
• Jihad Final Reckoning, Captured Ships (p. 129) — “Dark Claw | Essex (2750) | Necromo (3072)”
