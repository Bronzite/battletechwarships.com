[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Unfinished Draconis Combine battleship hull secretly transferred with the Yamato orbital yard from New Samarkand to Dover V in 3061 and converted over ten years with Clan Diamond Shark factory components into a massive LAW mobile factory ship. On 18 December 3099, while operating in Bikini Atoll's asteroid belt under the guard of DCS Amber Lotus, Yamato was pursued by CGB Rasalhague and two Stars of Dominion Vanir Pocket WarShips, disabled under capital missile fire within an hour, and accelerated into the Bikini Atoll star's corona, destroying her entirely.
