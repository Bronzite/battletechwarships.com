[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Congress-class WarShip whose keel was broken by the Wolverine flagship SLS Michigan during the Battle of Barbados on 27 June 2824 (an act that infuriated Smoke Jaguar Khan Osis); during Operation REVIVAL it later withdrew from combat after fighters from the carrier DCS Ashi-oto damaged it. Absent from the 3067 Field Manual: Updates Clan Jade Falcon naval roll; ultimate disposition unrecorded.
