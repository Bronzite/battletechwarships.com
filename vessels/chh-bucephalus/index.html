* 3136-04-03 // FLEETDEPLOYMENT
FACTION: Clan Hell's Horses | ROLE: FleetDeployment | SYSTEM: Sudeten | COMMANDER: Galaxy Commander Manas Amirault

Jumped to Sudeten's pirate point carrying Clan Hell's Horses' Fire Horse Galaxy and escorted Malvina Hazen's battle-damaged CJF Emerald Talon into orbital approach during the overthrow of Khan Jana Pryde.

• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 28 (3 April 3136) — “Standing beside Malvina Hazen on the Bucephalus's observation deck... Galaxy Commander Manas Amirault stood on Malvina's other side... following the wounded Emerald Talon toward Sudeten.”
• A Rending of Falcons, Chapter 29 (3 April 3136) — “"We have received independent communications from the Emerald Talon," the Hell's Horses aide went on. "A missile penetrated the combat information center. Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti has suffered severe injury."”
* 3136-04-03 // BATTLE OF SUDETEN & DESTRUCTION OF HAMMARR'S FALCON'S PERCH [SURVIVED]
FACTION: Clan Hell's Horses | ROLE: Allied Escort Frigate | SYSTEM: Sudeten | COMMANDER: Galaxy Commander Manas Amirault

Carried Fire Horse Galaxy through Sudeten's pirate point and screened Emerald Talon's approach with aerospace fighters until Malvina Hazen murdered Amirault on 4 April 3136 over Hammarr's destruction.
