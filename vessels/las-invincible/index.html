LCS Invincible - Lyran Commonwealth - - Original SLDF/Commonwealth hull designation; served as flagship of Allied liberation forces at Tharkad in January 3072 before joining Devlin Stone's Coalition.
[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Recovered Tharkad-class battlecruiser that vaporized the last Baron-class destroyer DCS Yedo at Hesperus in 2853, helped retake Tharkad from Word of Blake Pocket WarShips on 11 January 3072, and deployed in Task Force Earthbound during Operation SCOUR against Terra on 15 August 3078.

* 2853-10-25 // RELIEF
FACTION: Lyran Commonwealth | ROLE: Relief | SYSTEM: Hesperus II

Reactivated from mothballs over Tharkad with a veteran crew, jumped into Hesperus space on 25 October 2853, sank DCS Wyrm and DCS Yedo, bombarded DCMS positions from orbit, and vanished on a misjump returning to Tharkad.
