ALIAS / NAMEHULL NUMBEROPERATING FACTIONACTIVE YEARSREFIT DESIGNCONTEXT
LCS Invincible - Lyran Commonwealth - - Original SLDF/Commonwealth hull designation; served as flagship of Allied liberation forces at Tharkad in January 3072 before joining Devlin Stone's Coalition.
[ FINAL FATE & DISPOSITION ]

Recovered Tharkad-class battlecruiser that vaporized the last Baron-class destroyer DCS Yedo at Hesperus in 2853, helped retake Tharkad from Word of Blake Pocket WarShips on 11 January 3072, and deployed in Task Force Earthbound during Operation SCOUR against Terra on 15 August 3078. Absent from Field Manual: 3145, which identifies LCS Yggdrasil as House Steiner's sole remaining WarShip.

* 2853-10-25 // RELIEF
FACTION: Lyran Commonwealth | ROLE: Relief | SYSTEM: Hesperus II

Reactivated from mothballs over Tharkad with a veteran crew, jumped into Hesperus space on 25 October 2853, sank DCS Wyrm and DCS Yedo, bombarded DCMS positions from orbit, and vanished on a misjump returning to Tharkad.

• Second Succession War, Siege of Hesperus II (p. 75) — “the LCS Invincible skimmed the atmosphere and blasted the Combine positions... Unfortunately for the Lyrans, the Invincible would also join its Combine cousins by misjumping while en-route back to Tharkad.”

Sank DCS Wyrm and DCS Yedo on arrival, skimmed atmosphere to bombard DCMS forces, then misjumped en route back to Tharkad (resurfacing in 3067 under Word of Blake control).

* 3078-08-15 // OPERATION SCOUR: COALITION NAVAL ASSAULT ON TERRA & TITAN YARDS [DAMAGED]
FACTION: Lyran Alliance / Coalition | ROLE: Task Force Earthbound Battlecruiser & Command Ship | SYSTEM: Terra (Sol) | COMMANDER: Fleet Admiral Johanna Gaines

Committed alongside LAS Katherine Steiner and LAS Katrina Steiner in Task Force Earthbound; hosted Devlin Stone's 17 August 3078 D-Day broadcast and coordinated Coalition screening lanes and fighter cover under Fleet Admiral Johanna Gaines after CSV Invisible Truth fell, emerging so battered and aged she was fit only as a training or museum ship.
