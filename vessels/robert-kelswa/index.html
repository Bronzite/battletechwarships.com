* 3064-06-11 // FLEETENGAGEMENT
FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: FleetEngagement | SYSTEM: Halfway | COMMANDER: Admiral Maria Esteban

Served as Admiral Maria Esteban's Loyalist zenith-point flagship during the June 3064 assault on Halfway.

• Storms of Fate, Chapter Nine (11 June 3064) — “Dividing the assault force into two drives, Esteban had kept the LAS Robert Kelswa as her flagship... Esteban had jumped in at the zenith point.”
* 3066-08-24 // BATTLE OF THARKAD (WOLF-IN-EXILE & LYRAN ALLIANCE ENGAGEMENT) [CRIPPLED]
FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: Picket Corvette | SYSTEM: Tharkad

Endgame describes an unnamed Lyran Fox-class corvette disabling Kerensky's Blues before being gutted into an out-of-control spiral by Werewolf; Field Manual Updates records that LAS Robert Kelswa was damaged in the Battle of Tharkad, taken briefly as isorla, and kept out of service until at least 3070.

• Endgame, Chapter Eighteen — “Two heavy particle projection cannon carved long, blackened scars across the corvette's nose and down its port side... Then the Werewolf's main guns hammered into and through the softened metal, chewing into the Fox's flesh.”
• Field Manual Updates, Lyran Alliance: Ships of the Alliance (pp. 166-173) — “The Yggdrasil and the Robert Kelswa were briefly taken by the Wolf Clan (in-Exile) as isorla... because of damage sustained in the Battle of Tharkad, the Robert Kelswa will not reenter service until at least 3070.”
* 3066-08-24 // COMBATACTION
FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: CombatAction | SYSTEM: Tharkad

Sustained crippling damage in the Battle of Tharkad, taken briefly by Wolf Clan (in-Exile) as isorla, and returned to the LAAF under the Arc-Royal protocols with repairs projected until at least 3070.

• Field Manual Updates, Lyran Alliance: Ships of the Alliance (pp. 166-173) — “The Yggdrasil and the Robert Kelswa were briefly taken by the Wolf Clan (in-Exile) as isorla but returned to the LAAF by Khan Kell after the Arc-Royal protocols; because of damage sustained in the Battle of Tharkad, the Robert Kelswa will not reenter service until at least 3070.”
* 3066 // COMMISSIONING
FACTION: Lyran Alliance | ROLE: Commissioning | SYSTEM: Alarion

Evacuated near completion from Alarion prior to the Allied assault on the system.

• Storms of Fate, BattleTech Legends: Storms of Fate — “Cranston grimaced. 'The Robert Kelswa was the only WarShip close to completion, and it was removed prior to our arrival.'”
* 3069-04-12 // CAPTURE
FACTION: Word of Blake | ROLE: Capture | SYSTEM: Alarion

Pillaged from Alarion's shattered shipyards by Word of Blake WarShips while still undergoing repair.

• Jihad Hot Spots 3070, Alarion Raid (April 3069) — “Word of blake Warships destroyed the yards yesterday—after pillaging as many vessels as they could, including the still-damaged las Robert Kelswa.”
