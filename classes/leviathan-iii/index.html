[ CLASS SUMMARY // LEVIATHAN III (PRODUCTION CONFIGURATION) ]

Constructed at the Alshain Naval Yards under Scientist General Gwendolyn (Agassiz) from the late 3130s and revealed on the eve of the ilClan Era, the 2,400,000-ton Leviathan III represents the largest super-battleship conceived by the Rasalhague Dominion. Carrying 5,373 tons of Lamellor Ferro-Carbide armor, twenty DropShip docking collars, sixty fighter cubicles, and devastating Heavy NPPC, NL55, and Heavy N-Gauss batteries, the class pairs late-era Inner Sphere–Clan shipbuilding with twenty-four NL55 broadsides per flank.
