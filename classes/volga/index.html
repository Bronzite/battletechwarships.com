> CLASS DOSSIER // VOLGA

TRANSPORT // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE (2750)/CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // VOLGA ]

Built by Eaton AeroSpace on the hulls of mothballed Quixote-class frigates from 2709, the 780,000-ton Volga-class transport sacrificed drive thrust and multi-arc capital endurance to haul over 213,000 tons of cargo and four DropShips. More than 200 were lost during the Liberation of Terra due to poorly designed coolant loops, though roughly two dozen escaped on the Exodus and were refitted by the Clans with lithium-fusion batteries and expanded fighter bays.
