> CLASS DOSSIER // THARKAD

BATTLECRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // THARKAD ]

Designed in 2685 and launched from the Bolson-Tamar shipyards in 2690 with over twenty built before the First Succession War, the 900,000-ton Tharkad-class battlecruiser mounted Heavy Naval Gauss Rifles, Heavy NPPCs, NAC/35s, 983.5 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, six DropShip collars, and lithium-fusion batteries. After saving Hesperus II in 2853, the last survivor LCS Invincible vanished only to return in Word of Blake hands on 5 December 3067 before Allied forces retook her over Tharkad in January 3072.
