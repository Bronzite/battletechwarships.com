[ CLASS SUMMARY // KIMAGURE SURPRISE (LIGHT MASS DRIVER TESTBED) ]

Converted by SLDF Naval R&D under Jasper Cochrane at the Titan orbital yards in 2756 on the Kimagure-class cruiser SLS Surprise, this experimental refit lengthened the prow to 758 meters to mount a prototype Light Mass Driver and three aerospace squadrons. Although kinetic firing crippled internal junctions and warped twelve hull compartments, a planned structural redesign was cut short when Captain Belinda Pace scuttled the Surprise at Titan upon the outbreak of the Amaris Civil War.
