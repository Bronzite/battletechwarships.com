[ CLASS SUMMARY // NARUKAMI ]

Launched in 2770 in the wake of the Amaris Coup, the Block II Narukami-class destroyer substituted 500 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor for the original's Improved Ferro-Aluminum, freeing weight to replace medium laser secondary batteries with Particle Projector Cannons and reconfigure the hangar bay to carry 12 fighters and 2 small craft (BV 134,418). Alongside Samarkand II carriers, Block II Narukamis formed the core of the Combine's First Succession War strike groups.
