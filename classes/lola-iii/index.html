DESTROYER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // LOLA III ]

Introduced in 2662 by Bolan AeroSpace after solving the fusion drive cooling flaws of the earlier Lola I and Lola II classes, the 680,000-ton Lola III-class destroyer was the most numerous WarShip in the Star League Navy. Capable of 3.0G flank thrust and armed with ten Heavy Naval PPCs and nine Naval Laser 45s, it remained a mainstay of both ComStar and the Clans.
