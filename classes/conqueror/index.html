BATTLECRUISER // TECH BASE: CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // CONQUEROR ]

Converted by Clan Snow Raven from two Star League-era Kimagure-class pursuit cruisers taken on the Exodus as carrier-escorts for the aborted joint Leviathan dreadnought project. Retaining the Kimagure's signature hatchetface prow while sacrificing acceleration to house 100 aerospace fighters across twelve bay doors, two DropShip collars, 950 tons of Lamellor Ferro-Carbide armor, an onboard HPG (with 10 techs), and massive NAC/25, NAC/30, NPPC, and Barracuda batteries, CSR Conqueror launched in 3052 and CSR Ark Royal in 3059; both anchored Snow Raven naval stars from the Jihad through 3152.
