[ CLASS SUMMARY // KYUSHU ]

Constructed at the Terada WarShip Yard in the Dieron system beginning in 3057, the 630,000-ton Kyushu-class frigate marked the rebirth of the Draconis Combine Admiralty. Carrying four DropShips, 18 fighters, an integral BattleMech battalion bay, Heavy Naval PPCs, and four AR10 missile launchers, the DCS Haruna represented the Combine alongside Task Force Serpent.
