> CLASS DOSSIER // FENG HUANG

CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // FENG HUANG ]

Built secretly at Necromo's Aris Memorial Yards with Word of Blake technical support to counter Free Worlds League Thera carriers, the 970,000-ton Feng Huang-class cruiser pairs an ammunition-free all-energy arsenal of Heavy, Medium, and Light Naval PPCs and NL35/45/55 batteries with 1,692 tons of Lamellor Ferro-Carbide armor, a lithium-fusion battery, and 96 battle armor marines. Early Technical Readout: 3067 intelligence credited the class with 2.0G/3.0G thrust, K-F drive integrity 20, and 6 DropShip collars before Jihad Turning Points: Sian record sheets revised those to 1.0G/1.5G, K-F integrity 13, and 2 DropShip collars (with an Upgrade variant adding 123 fighters).
