TROOP CRUISER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // POTEMKIN ]

First deployed in 2611 and measuring 1,508 meters from bow to stern, the 1,510,000-ton Potemkin-class troop cruiser is the premier strategic invasion carrier of the Star League and Clan navies. Fitted with an unmatched 25 DropShip docking collars, a single Potemkin can transport an entire Star League division or Clan Galaxy into a contested star system in a single jump.
