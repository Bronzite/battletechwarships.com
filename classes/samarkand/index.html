> CLASS DOSSIER // SAMARKAND

CARRIER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // SAMARKAND ]

Fast-tracked into Draconis Combine service under Coordinator Hehiro Kurita in 2579 (and upgraded to Ferro-Carbide Block II specifications under Urizen II in 2668), the 350,000-ton Samarkand-class carrier fielded two full wings of 72 aerospace fighters and three DropShip collars. Exported widely to the SLDF and serving in all eight Combine fleets, most perished in the Liberation of Terra and early Succession Wars, leaving Togura as a museum piece orbiting New Samarkand.
