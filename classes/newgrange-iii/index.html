> CLASS DOSSIER // NEWGRANGE

YARDSHIP // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // NEWGRANGE ]

Spanning 2,680 meters and displacing 2.3 million tons, the Star League Newgrange-class YardShip—introduced in 2600 in roughly 200 hulls—featured a colossal bow unpressurized repair dock capable of servicing full WarShips up to 2.1 million tons. Only ~45 survived the Amaris War and Exodus, though one Word of Blake survivor, Blake's Mercy (later Republic ship Mercy), appeared at Coventry and relocated to Terra.
