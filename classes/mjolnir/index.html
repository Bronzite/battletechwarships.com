> CLASS DOSSIER // MJOLNIR

BATTLECRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // MJOLNIR ]

Built jointly by Bowie Industries and Ioto Galactic Enterprises at Alarion's Port Sydney orbital yards, the 1,350,000-ton Mjolnir-class battlecruiser was the largest WarShip constructed in the Inner Sphere following the fall of the Star League. Trading DropShip docking capacity for a larger fusion drive capable of 3.0G peak thrust, heavy Ferro-Carbide armor, and overwhelming close-range Naval Gauss, NAC, Heavy NPPC, and AR10 broadsides, the Mjolnir entered Lyran Alliance service during the FedCom Civil War with vessels such as LAS Yggdrasil and LAS Fylgia.
