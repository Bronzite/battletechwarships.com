[ CLASS SUMMARY // MJOLNIR ]

Built jointly by Bowie Industries and Ioto Galactic Enterprises from subcontracted Alliance components assembled at Alarion's Port Sydney naval yards, the Mjolnir-class battlecruiser (1,350,000 tons in AeroTech 2 Revised construction tables; listed as 1,250,000 tons with KF Drive 25 in Technical Readout: 3067 and Jihad Turning Points: Tharkad) was the largest WarShip constructed in the post-Star League Inner Sphere. Combining 3.0G peak thrust, 3,500 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, Heavy Naval Gauss, Heavy NPPC, NAC/30-40, and AR10 broadsides, two hulls entered service: LAS Yggdrasil and LAS Fylgia.
