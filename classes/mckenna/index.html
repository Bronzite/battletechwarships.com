[ CLASS SUMMARY // MCKENNA ]

Named after Admiral James McKenna, the founder of the Terran Hegemony, the 1,930,000-ton McKenna-class battleship represents the pinnacle of Star League naval architecture. Entering service in 2652, the McKenna served as the supreme command flagship of the Star League Defense Force Naval Command and later as the centerpiece of the Clan Exodus Fleet and Operation Klondike.
