TRANSPORT // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // ROBINSON ]

Debuting in 2560 after the Davion Civil War (with an upgraded Block II in 2705 mounting Ferro-Carbide armor), the 400,000-ton Robinson-class armored transport combined 36 integral BattleMech bays, orbital cocoon combat-drop capability, four DropShip collars, and 24 fighters. Two Block I hulls captured by Draconis Combine forces during the Succession Wars later inspired the Combine's Kyushu-class frigate.
