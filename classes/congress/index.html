FRIGATE // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // CONGRESS ]

Entering Terran Hegemony service in 2542 on the eve of the Reunification War, the 760,000-ton Congress-class frigate is a versatile multi-role capital ship. Armed with five White Shark antiship missile tubes, NAC/30 broadsides, and Naval Lasers while carrying two DropShips, it saw service with the SLDF, ComStar, the Free Worlds League, and multiple Clans.
