> CLASS DOSSIER // NIGHTWING

SURVEILLANCE // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // NIGHTWING ]

Developed by DiTron Heavy Industries of Terra in 2447 as a covert intelligence-gathering companion to the Tracker, the 100,000-ton Nightwing surveillance vessel mimicked civilian JumpShip silhouettes while hiding 75 tons of spy gear, four small craft bays, and capital PPC, laser, and NAC/20 batteries. Phased out after the arrival of the Bug-Eye, none were thought to have survived the Amaris crisis.
