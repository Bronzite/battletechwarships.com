> CLASS DOSSIER // VINCENT MK. 39

CORVETTE // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // VINCENT MK. 39 ]

First commissioned by the Terran Hegemony in 2432 to replace the Bonaventure and Vigilant patrol classes, over 500 modular 420,000-ton Vincent Mk. 39 corvettes entered service before the Amaris Coup. Armed with Barracuda launchers, NAC/10s, and Large Lasers—and clandestinely restarted in Capellan Delhi WarShips yards after 2766—the Mk. 39 served the SLDF and Great Houses and later became the basis for the Clan Mk. 42 upgrade.
