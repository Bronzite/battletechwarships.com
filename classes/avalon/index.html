CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE (FEDERATED SUNS)
[ CLASS SUMMARY // AVALON ]

Constructed at the McKenna Shipyards above Kathil by Kathil Shipworks, Universal Air, Federated-Boeing Interstellar, and General Motors, the 770,000-ton Avalon-class missile cruiser anchored the Federated Suns naval revival. Featuring a distinctive split-hull catamaran architecture, 10,000 tons of extended-range fuel bunkerage, six assault-DropShip collars, 1,771 tons of Lamellor Ferro-Carbide armor, Medium Naval Gauss broadsides, and eight AR10 launchers, multiple hulls clashed directly during the FedCom Civil War at Kathil, Tikonov, and New Avalon.
