> CLASS DOSSIER // AVALON

CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE (FEDERATED SUNS)
[ CLASS SUMMARY // AVALON ]

Constructed at the McKenna Shipyards in orbit over Kathil by a consortium of Kathil Shipworks, Universal Air, Federated-Boeing Interstellar, and General Motors, the 770,000-ton Avalon-class cruiser is the premier fleet flagship of the Federated Suns naval revival. Distinguished by its split-hull catamaran architecture, Naval Gauss Rifle broadsides, and AR10 capital missile launchers, several hulls clashed directly during the FedCom Civil War at Kathil, Tikonov, and New Avalon.
