[ CLASS SUMMARY // COMMONWEALTH (BLOCK I) ]

Born of financial maneuvering that spun Ioto Galactic Enterprises out of Di Tron Industries on Tharkad and built at Alarion's Port Sydney yards beginning in 2375, the 700,000-ton Commonwealth-class light cruiser packed six DropShip collars, Improved Ferro-Aluminum armor, and heavy NL55 and capital missile batteries; a 2765 Block II variant briefly escorted Tharkad battlecruisers into the early Succession Wars.
