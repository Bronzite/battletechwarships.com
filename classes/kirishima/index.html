CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE (DRACONIS COMBINE)
[ CLASS SUMMARY // KIRISHIMA ]

Commissioned by the Draconis Combine in 3061 following the lessons of Operation Bulldog, the 790,000-ton Kirishima-class cruiser is an exceptionally fast and heavily armed capital ship. Boasting a 4.0G maximum thrust, over 1,099 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, ten Heavy Naval PPCs, and fourteen capital missile launchers, it served as the premier combat flagship of the DCMS fleet.
