CORVETTE // TECH BASE: CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // MOLNIYA ]

Introduced in 2951 as Clan Wolf's first domestically designed WarShip, the 240,000-ton Molniya-class corvette traded heavy hull armor and capital energy weapons for five DropShip docking collars, twenty fighter cubicles, and AR-10 missile bays while relying on Snow Raven-built fusion engines. Difficult to maintain and lightly armored, the class suffered catastrophic losses at the 2966 Trial of Lum against Snow Raven carrier CSR Corvidae and was entirely retired before 3000.
