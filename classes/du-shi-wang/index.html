> CLASS DOSSIER // DU SHI WANG

BATTLESHIP // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // DU SHI WANG ]

Commissioned by the Duchy of Liao and launched with the founding of the Capellan Confederation in 2380, the 900,000-ton Du Shi Wang-class battleship relied on an ammunition-free all-energy arsenal anchored by four bow Heavy NPPCs and massive 240-point frontal armor. Noted for opening Age of War engagements and orbital bombardment at Novaya Zemlya in 2399, one captured hull—Jasmine Liao—became Clan Mongoose's flagship Quicksilver Mongoose after the Exodus.
