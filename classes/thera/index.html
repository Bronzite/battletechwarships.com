[ CLASS SUMMARY // THERA ]

Built by Illium Naval Engineering at Ionus with Word of Blake assistance, the 960,000-ton Thera-class super-carrier formed the heavily armored (1,700 tons Lamellor Ferro-Carbide) command core of Free Worlds League Navy battle groups, escorted by paired Eagle frigates and Hamilcar, Achilles, and Merlin assault DropShips. While its Technical Readout: 3067 stat block specifies 108 + 6 fighter cubicles (114 fighters) and 8 small craft, prose notes it was designed around four aerospace regiments (up to 216 fighters) alongside a 50-ton Mobile HPG (with 10 HPG techs), three grav decks, and 448 passenger berths. Six hulls—FWLS Santorini, Sardis, Corinth, Delphi, Delos (3068), and Thessaly (3070)—were built.
