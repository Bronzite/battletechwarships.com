> CLASS DOSSIER // FASLANE

YARDSHIP // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE (COMSTAR)
[ CLASS SUMMARY // FASLANE ]

Constructed in six hulls by ComStar between 2882 and 2949 based on the Star League Newgrange, the 1,810-meter Faslane-class YardShip housed a 2,000,000-ton-capacity unpressurized repair dock and a Mobile HPG. Deploying Rosslare and Glamorgan to support Explorer Corps missions while holding others at Luyten 68-28 and Ross 248, three survived the Jihad to join the Republic Navy at Terra.
