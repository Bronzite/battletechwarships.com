BATTLESHIP // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // ATREUS ]

Designed in the final decades of the Age of War and launched in 2552 as the backbone of Free Worlds League naval battle groups, the 1,100,000-ton Atreus-class battleship rivaled Hegemony Monsoons and Farraguts in broadside weight and Improved Ferro-Aluminum protection. Nearly two-thirds of the class served into the Star League collapse before the last hulls were lost fighting Lyran fighters in 2809 during the First Succession War.
