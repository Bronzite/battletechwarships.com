CORVETTE / RAIDER // TECH BASE: CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // FREDASA ]

First constructed by the Clans in 2962, the 180,000-ton Fredasa-class corvette (designated a raider by Inner Sphere naval analysts) is one of the fastest WarShips ever built, capable of 4.0G peak thrust. Combining a compact emergence signature, 20 aerospace fighters, a single DropShip collar, a nose NAC/40, and four Naval Laser 45s, it was deployed primarily by Clans Nova Cat, Ghost Bear, and Steel Viper; one captured hull, Kerensky's Blues, fought in the Battle of Tharkad for the Blue Star Irregulars.
