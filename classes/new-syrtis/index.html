> CLASS DOSSIER // NEW SYRTIS

CARRIER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // NEW SYRTIS ]

Initiated in 2546 under First Prince Alexander Davion and introduced in 2557, the 920,000-ton New Syrtis was a dedicated fleet aerospace carrier housing six full fighter wings (120 fighters) and 24 small craft with double flight crews for continuous jump-point superiority. Five saw heavy action in Case AMBER during the Reunification War—including the captured FSS Chesterton at Panpour—and the final hull, FSS Pleiades, retired during the Second Succession War.
