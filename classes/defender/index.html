BATTLECRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // DEFENDER ]

Debuting in 2360 in the Federated Suns arsenal during the early Age of War, the 960,000-ton Defender-class battlecruiser achieved an exceptional 4.0G peak thrust for a capital warship of its displacement, pairing heavy NAC/20 and NAC/35 broadside batteries with dense conventional autocannon point-defense arrays and four DropShuttle bays.
