> CLASS DOSSIER // CARRACK

TRANSPORT // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // CARRACK ]

Developed by the Terran Hegemony as a militarized evolution of the Lyran Sylvester-class freighter and operated across Great House and Clan navies for centuries, the 300,000-ton Carrack naval transport carried armed escorts' supplies, two DropShip docking collars, and 98-meter centrifugal grav decks. During the Clan Golden Century and subsequent eras, military Carracks formed the logistical backbone of Clan Toumans until Diamonds Shark and Nova Cat ventures stripped several hulls down into lightly armed 2985 merchantmen.
