[ CLASS SUMMARY // CARRACK ]

Developed by the Terran Hegemony as a militarized evolution of the Lyran Sylvester-class freighter and operated across Great House and Clan navies for centuries, the 300,000-ton Carrack naval transport carried armed escorts' supplies, two DropShip docking collars, and a 98-meter centrifugal grav deck. The modern Clan Carrack (entered service c. 2950) lacks a lithium-fusion battery or Ferro-Carbide armor, carrying only 70 tons of Standard armor paired with hull-sealant technology and a 50-ton Mobile HPG (with 10 technicians), and notably ferried Clan Ghost Bear's entire civilian population to the Inner Sphere alongside Diamond Shark and Nova Cat trading fleets.
