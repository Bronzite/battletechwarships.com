[ CLASS SUMMARY // CORONE ]

Built by Clan Snow Raven at the Lum Naval Yards in 2915, the 290,000-ton Corone-class destroyer ushered in a new generation of Clan WarShips tailored for solo Trial warfare. Carrying 20 aerospace fighters, four DropShip collars, an onboard HPG, and a lithium-fusion battery, the six Corones saw extensive service over Hellgate, Strato Domingo, and Bearclaw before serving as the design baseline for the York and Nightlord classes and retiring to mothballs in 2946.
