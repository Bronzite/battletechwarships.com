> CLASS DOSSIER // EAGLE

FRIGATE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // EAGLE ]

Introduced in 3059 by the Free Worlds League's Illium Naval Engineering as paired escorts for the Thera super-carriers, the 620,000-ton Eagle-class frigate mounted 450 tons of Standard armor, a lithium-fusion battery, a 50-ton Mobile HPG (with 10 techs), Heavy Naval Gauss nose firepower, dual twin NAC/35 broadside batteries, and layered point-defense arrays. Early Intelligence in Technical Readout: 3067 recorded 4 DropShip collars and a 14-point K-F drive before later Jihad Turning Points: New Avalon record sheets revised those to 0 DropShip collars and 12 K-F drive integrity.
