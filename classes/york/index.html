> CLASS DOSSIER // YORK

DESTROYER / CARRIER // TECH BASE: CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // YORK ]

Converted by the Clans in 2947 from the six surviving Star League Riga II-class destroyer/carrier hulls (reconciling earlier Inner Sphere TRO reports that attributed the chassis to the Carson class), the 600,000-ton York-class destroyer/carrier expanded its flight decks to house 50 OmniFighters (nearly two full aerospace Clusters) alongside two DropShip collars, Naval Lasers, and four AR10 capital missile launchers.
