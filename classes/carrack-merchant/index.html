[ CLASS SUMMARY // CARRACK (CLAN MERCHANT CONVERSION) ]

Converted in 2985 through a joint venture between the Merchant Castes of Clans Diamond Shark and Nova Cat, the Clan Merchant variant of the 300,000-ton Carrack replaced all capital weaponry and heavy armor with light standard-scale defensive batteries to expand cargo volume by nearly a third to 98,806.5 tons alongside a Star of Marine Elementals.
