> CLASS DOSSIER // CONGRESS

FRIGATE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // CONGRESS ]

Born of a 2540 legal settlement bringing Galax shipbuilder Challenge Systems into the Congress frigate program, the Federated Suns 'Davion' Congress D stripped advanced Hegemony sensors in favor of Ferro-Carbide armor and heavier NAC/30 and NAC/10 broadside batteries. Surviving into the early Succession Wars to escort New Syrtis carriers, the final Congress D perished in a Capellan minefield during the Second Succession War.
