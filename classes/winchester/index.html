CRUISER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // WINCHESTER ]

Constructed at the Illiushin shipyards of the Taurian Concordat beginning in 2364, the 740,000-ton Winchester-class cruiser served as the pride of the early Concordat space fleet. Combining 2.5G sprint acceleration, three DropShip collars, and twelve fighters, the surviving Winchesters fought until the last hulls fell to the SLDF Navy at the Battle of Robsart in 2581.
