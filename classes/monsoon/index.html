> CLASS DOSSIER // MONSOON

BATTLESHIP // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / TERRAN HEGEMONY
[ CLASS SUMMARY // MONSOON ]

Introduced in 2368 as the architectural blueprint for Terran Hegemony capital ships for four centuries, the 1,310,000-ton Monsoon-class battleship carried 2,356.5 tons of standard armor, 18 fighters, and six DropShip collars. Legendary hulls such as Barham, Richelieu, Thunderer, and Cochran fought through the Age of War and Reunification War before only ten received lithium-fusion retrofits and the final ship retired in 2668.
