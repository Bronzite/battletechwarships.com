[ CLASS SUMMARY // BUG-EYE ]

Disguised to resemble civilian Buccaneer or Sylvester DropShips while packing a sub-compact K-F drive and massive Naval Comm Scanner Suite, the tiny 6,100-ton Bug-Eye was the Terran Hegemony's and SLDF's premier clandestine reconnaissance WarShip. Capable of 4.0G sprint thrust and zero-grav continuous surveillance, the estimated 170-ship class vanished during the Amaris Coup and Exodus, as the Clans scorned stealth spy vessels.
