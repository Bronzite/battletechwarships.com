> CLASS DOSSIER // WAGON WHEEL

FRIGATE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE / PERIPHERY
[ CLASS SUMMARY // WAGON WHEEL ]

Fielded by the Taurian Concordat in 2570 as a Periphery answer to resurgent Inner Sphere capital hulls, the 650,000-ton Wagon Wheel-class frigate combined heavy NAC/35 broadsides and 3.0G maximum thrust with three massive spoked grav decks retained from its original transport specification. Only a handful were built before the Reunification War, and the destruction of two Wagon Wheels by Federated Suns WarShips in the 2573 Malagrotta Affair became an immediate flashpoint of the conflict.
