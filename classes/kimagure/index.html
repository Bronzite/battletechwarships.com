CRUISER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // KIMAGURE ]

Built by Krester Ship Construction in 2582 as a dozen high-speed pursuit cruisers nicknamed 'Hatchetface' for their distinctive prows, the 780,000-ton Kimagure achieved corvette-grade 4.0G sprint acceleration while packing Heavy and Medium Naval PPC batteries in seven firing arcs. Five hulls escaped Terra on the Exodus, two of which Clan Snow Raven rebuilt into the Conqueror-class.
