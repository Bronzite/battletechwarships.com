[ CLASS SUMMARY // RIGA II ]

Rushed into Star League service in 2747 after First Lord Simon Cameron's death, the 600,000-ton Riga II was an anomalous heavy escort destroyer/carrier featuring 604 tons of Lamellor ferro-carbide armor, lithium-fusion batteries, two DropShip collars, and two full fighter squadrons (36 fighters). Sixty hulls were built by December 2766; half perished fighting Periphery insurgents and Amaris' Caspar drones, twenty-two departed on the Exodus, and six Clan survivors were later rebuilt into the York class.
