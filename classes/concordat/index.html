> CLASS DOSSIER // CONCORDAT

FRIGATE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE (TAURIAN CONCORDAT)
[ CLASS SUMMARY // CONCORDAT ]

Developed by the Taurian Concordat in 2506 to bridge corvettes and destroyers, the 440,000-ton Concordat-class frigate served as the backbone of Periphery naval fleets for over a century. More than four dozen entered Taurian and Canopian service, mounting bow NL55 batteries and medium-caliber NAC/20s capable of challenging Star League battleships throughout the Reunification War before the last hulls perished in the First Succession War.
