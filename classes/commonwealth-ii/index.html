> CLASS DOSSIER // COMMONWEALTH

LIGHT CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // COMMONWEALTH ]

Revived by the Lyran Commonwealth in 2765 to escort Tharkad-class battlecruisers during pre-Coup naval rearmament, the Block II variant of the 700,000-ton Commonwealth-class light cruiser retained six DropShip collars and 700 tons of Improved Ferro-Aluminum armor while reconfiguring hangar bays for 18 aerospace fighters and 4 small craft, swapping White Shark tubes for triple Barracuda launchers, and upgrading point-defense machine guns to Anti-Missile Systems (BV 102,832).
