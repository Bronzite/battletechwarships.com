[ CLASS SUMMARY // DAVION ]

Authorized by First Prince Alexander Davion after purging the corruption that marred the 520,000-ton Block I, the 580,000-ton Block II Davion-class destroyer entered AFFS service in 2552 with 623.5 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, double heat sinks (1,000/2,000), 18 aerospace fighters, 56 marines, and two DropShip collars typically carrying Achilles or Vengeance assault DropShips. All Block II Davions perished in the Succession Wars.
