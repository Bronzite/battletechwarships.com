[ CLASS SUMMARY // LEVIATHAN ]

Authorized in 3044 by Ghost Bear Khans Nornian Tseng and Ursula Jorgensson and co-designed with Clan Snow Raven to surpass even the McKenna battleship, the 2,400,000-ton Leviathan initially entered service in 3055 with two partially armed hulls adapted as colossal armored heavy transports (414,530.5 tons cargo, 8 DropShip collars, 8 grav decks) to secretly evacuate the entire Clan Ghost Bear civilian populace to the Inner Sphere. Subsequent refits restored the class into full battleships (alongside Clan Steel Viper's Prime hull CSV Perigard Zalman) that spearheaded Ghost Bear campaigns at Luthien, Pesht, Odessa, Dyev, Dieron, Titan, and Terra.
