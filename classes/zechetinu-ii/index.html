[ CLASS SUMMARY // ZECHETINU II ]

Upgraded production variant of Illium Naval Engineering's 180,000-ton Zechetinu-class corvette with higher combat rating (46,730 BV). Seven vessels served in the pre-Jihad Free Worlds League Navy—Scorpion, Araneida, Acari, Ixodida, Opilione, Kustarachnae, and Haptopoda—and Coalition flagship ISS Excalibur fought in Operation SCOUR over Terra.
