CORVETTE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // ZECHETINU II ]

Missile-armed production variant of Illium Naval Engineering's 180,000-ton Zechetinu-class corvette replacing fore and aft NL35 batteries with White Shark launchers, adding quarter Barracuda tubes, reducing cargo capacity slightly from 7,588.5 to 7,004.5 tons per hold, and raising the gunnery complement to 28 (plus 10 HPG techs for its 50-ton Mobile HPG). Seven vessels served in the pre-Jihad Free Worlds League Navy—Scorpion, Araneida, Acari, Ixodida, Opilione, Kustarachnae, and Haptopoda—and Coalition flagship ISS Excalibur fought in Operation SCOUR over Terra.
