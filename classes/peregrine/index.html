> CLASS DOSSIER // PEREGRINE

CORVETTE // TECH BASE: CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // PEREGRINE ]

Introduced by Clan Jade Falcon in 2969 during the Golden Century as a pragmatic, fast orbital picket and scout WarShip, the 130,000-ton Peregrine combined 4.5G acceleration, a lithium-fusion battery, and two NAC/35 nose guns with ten fighters and one DropShip collar. Notable vessels included Green Kestrel at the 3001 Trial on Arcadia and the mysteriously vanished Dark Caste hunter Silver Merlin.
