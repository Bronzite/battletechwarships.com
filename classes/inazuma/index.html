CORVETTE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // INAZUMA ]

A 200,000-ton Draconis Combine Admiralty stealth-attack corvette constructed at the expanded orbital facilities of Midway Shipyards above Midway (and assembled on Dieron slipways). Designed for rapid 4.0G ambush strikes on DropShips and armed JumpShips, its cramped spartan interior forced enlisted ratings to 'hot-rack' bunks while the DCA rotated dual crews every four to six months. Four commissioned hulls served prior to the Jihad—Night of Agony, Winds of Heaven (surviving into 3145), Amber Lotus, and Swiping Claw—while a fifth unfinished hull was destroyed on its Dieron slipway by unknown saboteurs in 3065.
