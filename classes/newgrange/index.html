> CLASS DOSSIER // NEWGRANGE

YARDSHIP // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // NEWGRANGE ]

Entering Star League service in 2600 across roughly 200 hulls, the 2.3-million-ton, 2,680-meter Newgrange-class YardShip was a mobile drydock and manufacturing plant capable of enclosing WarShips up to 2.1 million tons in its unpressurized forward bay while berthing over 7,000 shipyard personnel. Roughly 45 survived the Amaris Civil War and Exodus; none were thought to survive the Succession Wars until Word of Blake deployed a Newgrange (Blake's Mercy) at Coventry, and ComStar reportedly modeled its smaller Faslane YardShips on two derelict Newgrange hulls.
