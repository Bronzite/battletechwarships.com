CARRIER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE (EXPERIMENTAL)
[ CLASS SUMMARY // ENTERPRISE ]

Conceived by Star League naval planners in 2745 and built by Daussault-Shimmon Enterprises over New Earth, the 1,600,000-ton Enterprise Super Carrier held eighteen aerospace wings (972 fighters) across 54 bay doors alongside a 2,500-track CIC and dense capital/AMS batteries. Incompatible mismatched schematics rendered the prototype's engines inoperable on launch in 2749, leaving the stripped hulk abandoned in the Sol asteroid belt.
