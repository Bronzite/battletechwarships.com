> CLASS DOSSIER // AEGIS

HEAVY CRUISER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // AEGIS ]

First launched by the Terran Hegemony in 2372 and upgraded continuously for over three centuries, the 750,000-ton Aegis-class heavy cruiser was one of the longest-serving capital ships in human history. With four DropShip collars, 20 aerospace fighters, heavy naval autocannons, and twelve capital missile launchers across its broadsides, it formed the backbone of SLDF, Clan, and Com Guard cruiser squadrons.
