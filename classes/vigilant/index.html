> CLASS DOSSIER // VIGILANT

CORVETTE // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // VIGILANT ]

Introduced in 2320 as one of the Terran Hegemony's earliest WarShip classes, the 140,000-ton Vigilant-class corvette paired in four-ship 'buddy' squadrons alongside Bonaventure sensor ships. Although lightly armored and hampered by primitive sensors, its heavy White Shark missile and NAC/10 armament kept sold-off hulls active in Great House service into the early Succession Wars.
