BATTLECRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // BLACK LION ]

Commissioned by Admiral James McKenna in 2315 as a faster, shared-component companion to the Dreadnought battleship, the 720,000-ton Black Lion I defined early Terran Hegemony battlecruiser doctrine. Sold to the Great Houses before the Reunification War once docking-collar designs superseded them, they inspired Boeing Interstellar's 2691 Black Lion battlecruiser.
