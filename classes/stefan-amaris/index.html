> CLASS DOSSIER // STEFAN AMARIS

BATTLESHIP // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // STEFAN AMARIS ]

Named for Rim Worlds Republic President Stefan Amaris and introduced in 2764, the 1,040,000-ton Stefan Amaris-class battleship was the longest WarShip ever constructed (1,605 meters). Armed with nine Heavy NPPCs, twin NAC/30 and NAC/25 broadsides, Killer Whale launchers, and 1,889 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, the first three hulls received Hegemony lithium-fusion batteries just prior to the 2766 Coup; two unfinished hulls captured by Kerensky in the Rim Worlds were completed as SLS Vengeance and SLS Chieftain, and none survived the Liberation of Terra.
