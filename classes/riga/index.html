> CLASS DOSSIER // RIGA

FRIGATE // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // RIGA ]

Built by Boeing Interstellar from 2440 through the launch of the Congress class in 2542, the 750,000-ton Riga-class frigate combined long-range Naval PPC and Laser energy batteries with heavy NAC/30 and NAC/25 autocannons and two DropShip collars. Serving extensively as extended-range patrol escorts through the Reunification War before being retired for Sovetskii Soyuz cruisers in 2735-2740, surviving hulls later served in the Periphery, Exodus, Operation Klondike, and the Word of Blake Navy.
