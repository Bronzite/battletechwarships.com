DESTROYER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // NAGA ]

Introduced in 2645 as a streamlined close-action light destroyer for the Terran Hegemony fleet, the 540,000-ton Naga-class combined two aerospace squadrons with standardized NAC/10, Light NPPC, and NL35 firepower. Displaced by the refitted Essex in 2711 and largely extinct after the First Succession War, three mothballed Sol hulls left orbiting Neptune were converted by the Word of Blake into Caspar II drone control ships.
