DESTROYER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // ESSEX I ]

Built by Delhi WarShips over Carver V starting in 2351, the 560,000-ton Essex I destroyer formed the Terran Hegemony's first-generation destroyer force alongside early Lolas through the Reunification War before decommission in 2645. During the Amaris Coup, Delhi WarShips smuggled blueprints to its Capellan shipyards to build unlicensed Essex Is that fought until 2830.
