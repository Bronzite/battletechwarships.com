[ CLASS SUMMARY // SUFFREN ]

Designed and built at ComStar's Titan Shipyards in the Terra system and entering Com Guard service in 3062, the 540,000-ton Suffren-class destroyer was ComStar's first newly constructed WarShip design since the fall of the Star League. Built to transport four DropShips and a reinforced company of 252 Level II Battle Armor marines, its lead ships SLS Anastasius Focht and SLS Manchester served in the Com Guard First and Second Fleets.
