> CLASS DOSSIER // SOYAL

HEAVY CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // SOYAL ]

Designed by Delhi WarShips in 2731 around an experimental bow-mounted Medium Mass Driver after losing the Star League tender to the Luxor, the 1,500,000-ton Soyal-class heavy cruiser entered service with the Capellan Confederation and Free Worlds League. Despite devastating opening kinetic salvos, its narrow forward firing arc left hulls vulnerable to faster WarShips during the Succession Wars.
