CORVETTE // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // ZECHETINU ]

A 180,000-ton Free Worlds League Navy convoy-raider, picket, and escort corvette manufactured by Illium Naval Engineering at Ionus with Word of Blake assistance. Fitted with 90 tons of Lamellor Ferro-Carbide armor, a lithium-fusion battery, an 88-meter grav deck, and a 50-ton onboard Mobile HPG operated by 10 HPG technicians (supplementing its stat-block crew, though prose reports 72 standard crew accommodations). Capable of 2.5G sustained and 4.0G sprint acceleration, the original Zechetinu I configuration armed four hulls—FWLS Tirana, FWLS Karelia, FWLS Attica, and FWLS Sparta.
