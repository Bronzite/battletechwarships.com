> CLASS DOSSIER // LEAGUE (BLOCK II)

DESTROYER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // LEAGUE (BLOCK II) ]

Driven by Captain-General Ewan Marik's naval expansion during the Amaris crisis and introduced in 2768, the Block II refit of the 550,000-ton League-class destroyer replaced its capital autocannons with Heavy, Medium, and Light Naval Gauss Rifles, upgraded armor to Improved Ferro-Aluminum, added a second DropShip collar, and berthed 56 marines. Chronic power shortages and scarce Gauss maintenance limited endurance, yet League Block IIs fought heavily in First Succession War actions off Second Chance and Corey (BV 53,315).
