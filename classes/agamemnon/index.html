HEAVY CRUISER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // AGAMEMNON ]

An 815,000-ton (stat-blocked as 820,000 tons in Technical Readout: 3067) Free Worlds League Navy heavy cruiser produced by Imstar Aerospace using zero-gravity refining facilities above Atreus. Trading heavy armor protection (735 tons Standard) for 3.0G agility, 18 aerospace fighters, heavy ER PPC anti-fighter defense, Heavy Naval Gauss broadsides, and onboard 50-ton Mobile HPGs operated by Word of Blake technicians (10 HPG techs), four hulls entered FWLN service—Menelaus (3058), Aineas/Aeneas (3060), Hippolyta (3063), and Troy (slated for trials in 3067-3068).
