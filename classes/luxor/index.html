> CLASS DOSSIER // LUXOR

HEAVY CRUISER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // LUXOR ]

Designed by Mitchell Vehicles to succeed the Avatar as the SLDF's mainline heavy cruiser from 2727 through 2765 (ending with Graham IV-built SLS Rhodan), the 890,000-ton Luxor combined 1,423 tons of armor with balanced NL55, Medium NPPC, Medium N-Gauss, and NAC/30 firepower. Employed as sacrificial Caspar-drone decoys to protect troop transports during the Amaris campaign, the last Luxors perished in the final battle for Terra.
