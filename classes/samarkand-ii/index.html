> CLASS DOSSIER // SAMARKAND

CARRIER // TECH BASE: INNER SPHERE
[ CLASS SUMMARY // SAMARKAND ]

Authorized by Coordinator Urizen II Kurita in 2668, the Block II upgrade of the 350,000-ton Samarkand-class carrier incorporated 511 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, nearly doubled heat-sink capacity (1,700), added a fourth DropShip docking collar and a full company of 100 marines, and up-gunned its capital batteries with twin Medium NPPCs, Heavy NPPC broadsides, and NAC/20s while retaining its two full wings of 72 fighters. Six of the DCA's eight Samarkands in 2765 were Block IIs.
