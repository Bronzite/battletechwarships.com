> CLASS DOSSIER // WHIRLWIND

DESTROYER // TECH BASE: STAR LEAGUE / CLAN
[ CLASS SUMMARY // WHIRLWIND ]

Entering Star League service in 2606, the 520,000-ton Whirlwind-class destroyer is recognizable by its flat, blunt arrowhead silhouette and 3.0G maximum acceleration. Equipped with NAC/30s, Light Naval PPCs, and a nose-mounted Killer Whale capital missile launcher, it saw extensive service in the SLDF, the Clan Toumans, and the Com Guard fleet.
