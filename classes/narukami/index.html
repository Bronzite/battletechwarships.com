[ CLASS SUMMARY // NARUKAMI ]

Built by the Draconis Combine in 2380 (and relaunched as a 2770 Ferro-Carbide Block II with a 12-fighter hangar), the 670,000-ton Narukami destroyer paired 4.0G sprint thrust and Heavy NPPC bow punch with fragile stress tolerances that required frequent Pesht shipyard maintenance before the final mothballed hulls were scrapped prior to the Jihad.
