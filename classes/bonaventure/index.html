[ CLASS SUMMARY // BONAVENTURE ]

Begun in the final years of the Terran Alliance and rushed into service by Director-General James McKenna in 2317, the 240,000-ton Bonaventure-class corvette paired 3.0G sprint capability with one of the twenty-fourth century's most sophisticated sensor suites. After proving lightly gunned in the Capella campaign, Hegemony planners teamed Bonaventures in pairs with heavily armed Vigilant corvettes for deep reconnaissance sweeps and border picket duty until the Vincent-class replaced them from 2432.
