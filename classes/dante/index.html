[ CLASS SUMMARY // DANTE ]

Built secretly at ComStar's renovated Titan shipyards between 2935 and 2949 to field heavily automated frigates requiring minimal crews, three 610,000-ton Dante-class hulls—Narbonne (2941), Bordeaux (2944), and Montpellier (2948)—combined 4.0G peak acceleration, 600 tons of Ferro-Carbide armor, a lithium-fusion battery, an onboard 50-ton Mobile HPG (with 10 technicians), and heavy NAC/30 and NAC/35 firepower.
