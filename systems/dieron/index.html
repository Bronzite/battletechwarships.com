[ SYSTEM DOSSIER // DIERON ]

Strategic district capital of the Draconis Combine and orbital anchor of the Terada WarShip Yard and Stellar Trek Enterprises facilities, building Kyushu-class frigates, Kirishima-class cruisers, Tatsumaki-class destroyers, and Inazuma-class corvettes. Unknown saboteurs destroyed a fifth unfinished Inazuma hull and its construction slipway here in 3065 before repairs reopened the line in late 3066.
