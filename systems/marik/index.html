[ SYSTEM DOSSIER // MARIK ]

Former Marik-Stewart Commonwealth world and capital of the Clan Protectorate. On 27 September 3136, Spina Khanate Delta Aimag CargoShip CSF Voidswimmer carried Star Colonel Rikkard Nova Cat and his Spirit Cat expedition to Marik's zenith jump point to claim the world, cementing the Spirit Cats and Sea Foxes as founders of the Clan Protectorate.
