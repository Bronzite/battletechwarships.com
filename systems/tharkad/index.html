> SYSTEM DOSSIER // THARKAD

AFFILIATION: LYRAN COMMONWEALTH / LYRAN ALLIANCE // CARTOGRAPHIC ARCHIVE
[ SYSTEM DOSSIER // THARKAD ]

Capital world of the Lyran Commonwealth and Lyran Alliance, located in the Donegal Province and home to the Royal Court in Tharkad City and the Nagelring military academy. Its orbit hosted the 24 August 3066 naval clash between Khan Phelan Kell's Wolf-in-Exile task force and Lyran Alliance defenders, and later the 3071-3072 Allied liberation from Word of Blake occupation.
