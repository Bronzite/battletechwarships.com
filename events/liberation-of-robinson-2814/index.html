> ENGAGEMENT LOG // NAVAL LIBERATION OF ROBINSON

DATE: 2814 // SYSTEM: ROBINSON
[ ACTION LOG // NAVAL LIBERATION OF ROBINSON ]

In March 2814, Admiral Mateo Arrian led Congress-D frigate FSS Starlight Raider, Davion II destroyers Duke Alexander and Sarah Davion, and carrier DropShips carrying Land-Air BattleMechs against the defending Samarkand II-class carrier DCS New Samarkand and Narukami II destroyer DCS Showakusei over Robinson.
