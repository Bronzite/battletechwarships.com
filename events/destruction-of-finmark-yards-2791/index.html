> ENGAGEMENT LOG // RAID ON FINMARK SHIPYARDS (OPERATION HAIFISCHFLOSSENSUPPE)

DATE: 2791 // SYSTEM: FINMARK
[ ACTION LOG // RAID ON FINMARK SHIPYARDS (OPERATION HAIFISCHFLOSSENSUPPE) ]

A Lyran Commonwealth naval task force under Kommodore Sharae (flagship Constantius, unclassed) assaulted the Rim Worlds Republic shipyards at Finmark. Defenders triggered nuclear charges in the main docking bay and launched surface-to-orbit strategic nuclear missiles, destroying LCS Potsdam and LCS Ulm and triggering retaliatory Commonwealth orbital bombardment of Finmark.
