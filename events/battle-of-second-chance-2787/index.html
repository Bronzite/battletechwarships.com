> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF SECOND CHANCE (PATH TO SARNA OPENING)

DATE: 2787 // SYSTEM: SECOND-CHANCE
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF SECOND CHANCE (PATH TO SARNA OPENING) ]

In February 2787, FWLN Atreus-class battleship Sorunda and League II destroyers Ospina and Otavalo jumped to Second Chance against the Sarna Commonality Squadron's Du Shi Wang-class CCS Mica Liao and Soyal-class cruiser CCS Solstice. Executing an in-system intercept jump from zenith, Solstice opened fire at extreme range with her mass driver.
