> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF WAYSIDE V (WILDCAT)

DATE: 3058-08-08 // SYSTEM: WAYSIDE-V
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF WAYSIDE V (WILDCAT) ]

Lured into the isolated Deep Periphery world of Wayside V (code-named Wildcat) by Northwind Highlanders operatives, Star Colonel Santin West's Sigma Galaxy of Clan Nova Cat clashed with Clan Smoke Jaguar's hidden Tau ('Huntress') Galaxy on 8 August 3058. In orbit above the surface battle, the Smoke Jaguar Essex-class destroyer Dark Claw (already sensor-damaged a month earlier by explosive life pods from DropShip Bull Run) engaged Clan Nova Cat WarShips, destroying the unnamed command WarShip of the Nova Cat Mystic Naval Star before suffering disabling damage.
