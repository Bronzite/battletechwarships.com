> ENGAGEMENT LOG // TRIAL OF LUM (MOLNIYA FLOTILLA ACTION)

DATE: 2966 // SYSTEM: LUM
[ ACTION LOG // TRIAL OF LUM (MOLNIYA FLOTILLA ACTION) ]

In 2966 Clan Wolf jumped four of its six Molniya-class corvettes to Lum's L1 point in a bid to seize a Snow Raven WarShip fusion-engine factory station as isorla. Forewarned by clumsy Wolf merchant inquiries, Clan Snow Raven bid down to the brand-new York-class carrier CSR Corvidae and a Delta Galaxy fighter Cluster. Charging through Wolf defensive formations, Corvidae's fighters bombed the thin-skinned corvettes while Corvidae braked into range and lashed them with naval autocannons.
