> ENGAGEMENT LOG // TRIALS FOR THE LUM NAVAL SHIPYARDS

DATE: 3068 // SYSTEM: LUM
[ ACTION LOG // TRIALS FOR THE LUM NAVAL SHIPYARDS ]

In December 3068, Clan Star Adder's Delta Naval Star attacked Lum in a massive six-Star fleet clash that crippled CSA Cho Polu and left the Snow Raven defenders reeling; immediately upon the Adders' departure, Clan Goliath Scorpion's Beta Naval Star under Galaxy Commander Colin Yeh jumped in and bid for the Lum Naval Yards.
