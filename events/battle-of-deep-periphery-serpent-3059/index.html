> ENGAGEMENT LOG // AMBUSH ON THE EXODUS ROAD (DEEP PERIPHERY)

DATE: 3059-12-15 // SYSTEM: DEEP-PERIPHERY-SERPENT
[ ACTION LOG // AMBUSH ON THE EXODUS ROAD (DEEP PERIPHERY) ]

While recharging at an uninhabited Deep Periphery star system en route to Huntress, Task Force Serpent's five-WarShip squadron was unexpectedly joined at the jump point by a migrating three-ship Clan Ghost Bear naval flotilla. To preserve the absolute secrecy of Operation Serpent, Commodore Alain Beresick ordered an immediate attack to prevent any Ghost Bear vessel from escaping.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: Total Task Force Serpent victory; the Ghost Bear frigate Shining Claw and destroyer Ursus were destroyed, while the destroyer Fire Fang was boarded and captured intact by the FCS Antrim and prize crews, adding a sixth WarShip to Task Force Serpent.
