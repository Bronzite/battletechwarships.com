> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF CHOLAME

DATE: 2790-03-09 to 2790-04-17 // SYSTEM: CHOLAME
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF CHOLAME ]

Expecting an abandoned rendezvous system after a two-month offensive sweep, Admiral Kenneth Jones jumped half of his Federated Suns armada directly into Tai-sho Isoru Khalfani's concealed Draconis Combine fleet anchorage on 9 March 2790. Using his flagship FSS Golden Lion's mobile HPG to redirect Vice Admiral Marjorie Stone's approaching second battle group, Jones ordered JumpShips scuttled while his WarShips waged a six-week running battle across Cholame to cover surviving transports.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: A Pyrrhic tactical victory for House Kurita that devastated both fleets: the Federated Suns lost 92 WarShips (including FSS Golden Lion) and 26 JumpShips, while the Combine lost 83 WarShips and 19 transport JumpShips, stalling DCMS offensive logistics for over fourteen months. Admiral Jones received the Medal Excalibur posthumously; Tai-sho Khalfani committed seppuku on 30 April 2790.
