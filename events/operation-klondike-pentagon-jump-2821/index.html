> ENGAGEMENT LOG // OPERATION KLONDIKE: PENTAGON NAVAL LIBERATION

DATE: 2821-07-01 // SYSTEM: PENTAGON-WORLDS
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION KLONDIKE: PENTAGON NAVAL LIBERATION ]

Following ilKhan Nicholas Kerensky's synchronized jump order from Way Station 7 on 30 June 2821, the newly formed Clan fleets jumped simultaneously into the five Pentagon Worlds on 1 July 2821 to seize the orbital mothball caches and crush rebel and spacefarer naval resistance.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: Decisive Clan naval victory across all five Pentagon star systems; the Arcadian and Babylonian mothball caches were secured intact, the rebel battleship Perth surrendered, the frigate Admiral Schone was destroyed, and only the Widowmaker destroyer Black Venom was lost and the Cloud Cobra cruiser Inquisitor crippled.
