DATE: 3060-03-05 // SYSTEM: HUNTRESS
[ ACTION LOG // FIRST BATTLE OF HUNTRESS (TASK FORCE SERPENT ARRIVAL) ]

Following DEST insertion aboard DCS Haruna's drop craft Stiletto to disable Huntress' Space Defense System, Task Force Serpent jumped into Huntress' zenith point on 5 March 3060 and clashed with a waiting Smoke Jaguar picket of one Sovetskii Soyuz-class heavy cruiser and two Vincent-class corvettes (PL 01 VNCT and PL 02 VNCT).
