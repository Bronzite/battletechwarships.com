[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF CHESTERTON (OPERATION CELT) ]

During Operation CELT in 2828, unclassed Capellan WarShips CCS Poznan and CCS Prominence initially secured orbit over Chesterton for invading Tikonov Lancers until Federated Suns WarShips FSS Nathan DuVall and FSS Pleiades (2828 hull) arrived during the CCAF withdrawal.
