DATE: 3077-11-20 // SYSTEM: DIERON
[ ACTION LOG // NAVAL BATTLE OF DIERON (20 NOVEMBER 3077) ]

Supporting the Ghost Bear assault on Dieron in late 3077, Star Admiral James Lankenau's Conqueror-class carrier CSR Ark Royal joined Star Admiral Gilmour's Leviathan-class flagship CGB Leviathan and Nightlord-class battleship CGB Ursa Major. On 20 November 3077, six Word of Blake and Free Worlds League Navy WarShips—FWLN Sardis, FWLN Aineas, FWLN Mordred, FWLN Tristram, WoBS End of Wisdom, and WoBS Light of Glory—approached under heavy EMCON and assaulted the Coalition fleet in a two-hour engagement.
