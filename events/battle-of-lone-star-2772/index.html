> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE FOR LONE STAR SPACE DEFENSE SYSTEM

DATE: 2772-07 // SYSTEM: LONE-STAR
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE FOR LONE STAR SPACE DEFENSE SYSTEM ]

In July 2772, Admiral Vicho Nnamani led a combined SLDF Eighth and Thirteenth Fleet task force of roughly seventy WarShips (expanding with XLIV Corps escorts to some sixty WarShips and over two hundred DropShips) into the first full-scale Star League assault on an Amaris-held Space Defense System at Lone Star. After smashing sixty-two Caspar drones at the zenith jump point at a cost of over a third of his force and wiping out an intercepting nadir drone wave en route from Vespa, Nnamani closed on Lone Star's final forty Caspars and three defense stations.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: Focusing on a single defense station to draw out the defenders, Nnamani's flagship SLS Quéribus was singled out and destroyed by Caspar drone WarShips early in the final melee, killing the Admiral. Vice Admiral Noeki Giersson assumed command aboard the McKenna-class SLS Sovereign Justice, directing the destruction of the remaining drone fleet and the capture or destruction of the three SDS stations to clear XLIV Corps' 25 July landings.
