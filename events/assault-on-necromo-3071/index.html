DATE: 3071-08-01 to 3071-08-28 // SYSTEM: NECROMO
[ ACTION LOG // ASSAULT ON NECROMO (DESTRUCTION OF ARIS MEMORIAL SHIPYARDS) ]

In August 3071, the Word of Blake's previously unknown Forty-eighth Shadow Division (Rimmon's Ravagers) struck Necromo days before an asteroid bombardment and bio-weapon release devastated the surface. Blakist aerospace forces neutralized the Aris Memorial Shipyards' orbital defenses and engaged the Capellan defense squadron, destroying CCS Sundermann Rhys and CCS Anhui while losing WBS Dark Claw to Capellan naval fire.
