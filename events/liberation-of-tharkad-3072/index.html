> ENGAGEMENT LOG // LIBERATION OF THARKAD & CAPTURE OF LCS INVINCIBLE

DATE: 3072-01-11 // SYSTEM: THARKAD
[ ACTION LOG // LIBERATION OF THARKAD & CAPTURE OF LCS INVINCIBLE ]

In January 3072 General Adam Steiner led a coalition relief force backed by twin Mjolnir battlecruisers LAS Fylgia and LAS Yggdrasil plus Fox-class LAS Ian McQuiston to break the Blakist naval blockade around Tharkad. While Fylgia and Yggdrasil engaged the Word-held Tharkad-class battlecruiser LCS Invincible, twenty assault shuttles delivered two hundred marines to retake the flagship deck-by-deck; meanwhile Ian McQuiston cleared Pocket WarShips before falling to nuclear missiles.
