> ENGAGEMENT LOG // ADDER'S ASSAULT: RECLAMATION OF TANIS

DATE: 3074-12-17 // SYSTEM: TANIS
[ ACTION LOG // ADDER'S ASSAULT: RECLAMATION OF TANIS ]

On 17 December 3074, Clan Star Adder Khan Stanislov N'Buta and Cloud Cobra allies arrived at the Tanis L1 point with CSA Stellar Serpent, CSA Divine Conquest, CSA Exodus Ranger, CCC Hell Fury, and CCC Brimstone to crush reborn Clan Burrock and Dark Caste forces defended by CBR Ingrid Bucharev and Second Coming.
