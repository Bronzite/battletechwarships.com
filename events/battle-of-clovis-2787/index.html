> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE FOR CLOVIS

DATE: 2787-05-01 // SYSTEM: CLOVIS
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE FOR CLOVIS ]

Supporting Warlord Minoru Kurita's flank assault on Clovis in May 2787, a DCA Third Fleet task force of two Samarkand carriers, four Narukami destroyers, and an Aegis cruiser battled three Federated Suns WarShips for six hours near lunar orbit before reinforcement carrier FSS New Ivaarsen arrived too late to avert Clovis' fall.
