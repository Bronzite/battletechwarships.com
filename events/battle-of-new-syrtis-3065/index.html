[ ACTION LOG // ORBITAL CLASH OVER NEW SYRTIS ]

On 21 October 3065, Katherine Steiner-Davion's Loyalist task force reinforced by the Avalon-class cruiser FCS Hanse Davion arrived in the New Syrtis system to compel Duke George Hasek's submission. Duke Hasek's defending Kirishima-class cruiser FCS Covenant closed to engage.
