DATE: 3151-03-01 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // THE DEATH RIDE OF THE REPUBLIC (TASK FORCE WALKÜRE OVER GENEVA) ]

Ordered by Exarch Devlin Stone to halt Jade Falcon orbital bombardments over Geneva at any cost, Admiral Jean-Jacques Labbé led Task Force Walküre (RSS Abundantia, RSS Redemption, field-patched RSS Shield of the Republic and RSS Triumphus, plus assault DropShip Ciaravella and five fellow DropShips) in a desperate braking charge on 1 March 3151 against CJF Turkina's Pride, CJF Blue Talon, CJF Jade Aerie, CJF White Aerie, and CJF Jade Tornado.
