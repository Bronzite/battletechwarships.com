> ENGAGEMENT LOG // OPERATION BIRTHRIGHT: BATTLE OF TERRA (ZENITH JUMP POINT)

DATE: 3151-01-21 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION BIRTHRIGHT: BATTLE OF TERRA (ZENITH JUMP POINT) ]

On 21 January 3151 (T+11), Khan Malvina Hazen jumped Clan Jade Falcon's invasion fleet into Terra's zenith jump point to begin Operation Birthright. Immediately engaged by Republic orbital fortresses Fort Grec and Fort Abernathy as well as Admiral Labbe's regrouped Task Force Atilla—including RSS Abundantia—the Falcons fought a brutal duel across the jump point while CJF Jade Aerie and CJF Blue Talon broke off to shield transport DropShips from RAF aerospace strikes.
