> ENGAGEMENT LOG // NAVAL BATTLE OF RIDGEBROOK

DATE: 2578-05-15 // SYSTEM: RIDGEBROOK
[ ACTION LOG // NAVAL BATTLE OF RIDGEBROOK ]

Opening I Corps landings in the Taurian Concordat on 15 May 2578, elements of the SLDF Third Battle Fleet clashed for five hours in Ridgebrook orbit with a Taurian WarShip squadron using moons and satellites for cover. Four Taurian WarShips were destroyed in exchange for three SLDF WarShips and ten troop DropShips carrying over a regiment of troops.
