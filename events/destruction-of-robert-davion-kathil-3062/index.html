> ENGAGEMENT LOG // LOSS OF FCS ROBERT DAVION OVER KATHIL

DATE: 3062-12-07 // SYSTEM: KATHIL
[ ACTION LOG // LOSS OF FCS ROBERT DAVION OVER KATHIL ]

During the opening flashpoint of the FedCom Civil War on 7 December 3062, Loyalist Admiral Jonathan Kerr commandeered the newly completed 800-meter Avalon-class battlecruiser FCS Robert Davion from McKenna Shipyards and dipped low along Kathil's atmosphere to intercept inbound Wolf's Dragoons transports. As Kerr prepared Killer Whale and Barracuda salvos against an Overlord DropShip, the unmanned, crippled Excalibur-class assault DropShip Guardian sheared clear of its towing tug Octopus on a gravity-bent ballistic trajectory toward Robert Davion.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: Robert Davion's hurried broadside barely deflected the 16,000-ton Guardian, which rammed the battlecruiser amidships and snapped its spine. Dragged into Kathil's upper atmosphere by its twisted drive section, Robert Davion broke into three primary fragments that struck the ocean southeast of Thespia (raising a three-meter coastal wave), the Iron-back Mountains in Muran (triggering Mount Daffyd's eruption), and the Thespia desert.
