> ENGAGEMENT LOG // MALAGROTTA INCIDENT (BATTLE OVER FONTANA)

DATE: 2573-02-19 // SYSTEM: FONTANA
[ ACTION LOG // MALAGROTTA INCIDENT (BATTLE OVER FONTANA) ]

After electrical and communications faults forced four Taurian Concordat WarShips and an auxiliary vessel to approach the jointly held mining moon of Fontana in the Malagrotta system for repairs on 19 February 2573, Federated Suns miners dispatched an emergency courier. An AFFS ten-ship WarShip task force approached covertly from a LaGrange jump point two days later and ambushed the Taurian flotilla in a fierce three-hour battle before it could launch fighters or DropShips.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: The Federated Suns lost no WarShips while destroying the Wagon Wheel-class frigates TCS Anaheim and TCS Cyrton and capturing TCS New Karachi and TCS Ulysses Run, leaving only TCS Pinard to escape. Rather than cowing the Concordat, the Malagrotta Incident inflamed Periphery resistance and directly paved the way toward the Reunification War.
