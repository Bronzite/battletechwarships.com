[ ACTION LOG // HANSEATIC-DIAMOND SHARK CLASH NEAR CHAINELAINE ISLES ]

In 3055, a Hanseatic League merchant-captain near the Chainelaine Isles attempted to enforce Hanseatic travel restrictions by harassing and chasing off a lone Clan Diamond Shark Merchant Caste JumpShip. Scarcely had the Hanseatic squadron celebrated when a Clan Diamond Shark WarShip jumped into the system to demonstrate the Clan's commitment to unrestricted Deep Periphery trade.
