DATE: 2819 // SYSTEM: KALIDASA
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE AT KALIDASA'S ZENITH JUMP POINT ]

Arriving at Kalidasa's zenith jump point in April 2819 during Operation SPIDERWEB, Lyran Tharkad-class battlecruisers LCS Ineffable and LCS Chaffee, two destroyers, and twelve assault DropShips intercepted an evacuating Marik convoy guarded by two Soyal-class cruisers and two corvettes.
