> ENGAGEMENT LOG // PURSUIT AT EPSILON ERIDANI (COURSING OF THE COM GUARDS)

DATE: 3141-01-02 // SYSTEM: EPSILON-ERIDANI
[ ACTION LOG // PURSUIT AT EPSILON ERIDANI (COURSING OF THE COM GUARDS) ]

On 2 January 3141, the Republic of the Sphere dispatched Aegis-class heavy cruiser RSS Auspicium and Lola III-class destroyer RSS Triumphus from Sol to hunt down Precentor Malcolm Buhl's fugitive Com Guard First Division. Arriving at Epsilon Eridani's nadir jump point, Auspicium and its Schrack fighter escorts intercepted Acolytes of Vision III-gamma's transport convoy, employing proximity K-F interference to pin irreplaceable JumpShips while allowing the WarShips' fighters and DropShips to cut off the Com Guards' escape.
