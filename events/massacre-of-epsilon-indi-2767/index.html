[ ACTION LOG // LOSS OF THE 568TH FLOTILLA AT EPSILON INDI ]

Acting immediately after news of Amaris's coup without waiting for General Kerensky, the 34th Royal BattleMech Division and Admiral Amanda Braso's 568th Armed Transport Flotilla (1 Black Lion, 2 Aegis, 4 Lola, 1 Potemkin, 20 transports) jumped into Epsilon Indi on 1 July 2767, only to find the Hegemony SDS reprogrammed against them and 56 M-5 Caspar drone WarShips waiting.
