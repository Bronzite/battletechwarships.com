DATE: 2777-03-11 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // ORBITAL BOMBARDMENT OF SANDHURST CASTLE BRIAN (SLS WELLESLEY) ]

After six weeks of costly attrition outside the Sandhurst Castle Brian fortress following the 30 January 2777 Operation LIBERATION landings, SLDF forces pulled back from the outer perimeter on 11 March 2777 while the Star League WarShip SLS Wellesley executed a devastating hour-long orbital bombardment against the Windsor Ride gate complex.
