DATE: 2832-04-04 // SYSTEM: TIKONOV
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION LION (FIRST BATTLE OF TIKONOV) ]

On 4 April 2832 a large Federated Suns invasion flotilla jumped into Tikonov's nadir point. Two docked Capellan frigates, CCS Jiaxing and CCS Zhaoqing, performed emergency separations from the recharge station and engaged the Davion assault marine transports.
