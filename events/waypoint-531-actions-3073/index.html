> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BANDIT & SCORPION ACTIONS AT WAYPOINT 531

DATE: 3073-07-01 // SYSTEM: WAYPOINT-531
[ ACTION LOG // BANDIT & SCORPION ACTIONS AT WAYPOINT 531 ]

In July 3073, Dark Caste bandit leader 'Jaguar' aboard Mako-class corvette Fuchida captured Precentor Gillick's Word of Blake depot JumpShips at Waypoint 531. In November 3073, Star Colonel Enrique Yeh's CGS Andromeda defeated Fuchida to claim the Blakist cache before saKhan Rood's task force fought CSJ Streaking Mist at the cache in June 3074.
