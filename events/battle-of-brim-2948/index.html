DATE: 2948-01-01 // SYSTEM: BRIM
[ ACTION LOG // COYOTE-CLOUD COBRA SPACE BATTLES AT BRIM ]

During Clan Coyote's 2948-2949 punitive assaults against Clan Cloud Cobra across Homer and Brim, Coyote Goth pilot Star Commander Cameron Smith ran sustained electronic warfare interference that created the opening for Coyote forces to disable the Cloud Cobra Potemkin-class troop cruiser CCC Wisdom of the Ages over Brim before Cloud Cobra Goth drop-ship hunters choked off Coyote resupply.
