> ENGAGEMENT LOG // CANOPIAN COUNTERATTACK AT VAKAREL

DATE: 2577-11-14 // SYSTEM: VAKAREL
[ ACTION LOG // CANOPIAN COUNTERATTACK AT VAKAREL ]

Three days after SLDF VII Corps declared Vakarel secured on 11 November 2577, the massed Magistracy of Canopus navy counterattacked dispersed Star League detachments amid the system's asteroid belts, destroying five JumpShips, a WarShip corvette escort, and two battalions of the Fifty-ninth Brigade before SLDF firepower drove them off.
