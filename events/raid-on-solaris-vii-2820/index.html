> ENGAGEMENT LOG // RETALIATORY RAID & BOMBARDMENT OF SOLARIS VII

DATE: 2820 // SYSTEM: SOLARIS
[ ACTION LOG // RETALIATORY RAID & BOMBARDMENT OF SOLARIS VII ]
