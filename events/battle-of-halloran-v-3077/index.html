> ENGAGEMENT LOG // CAPELLAN ASSAULT ON HALLORAN V (LOSS OF CSS FRANCO MARTELL)

DATE: 3077-02-11 // SYSTEM: HALLORAN-V
[ ACTION LOG // CAPELLAN ASSAULT ON HALLORAN V (LOSS OF CSS FRANCO MARTELL) ]

Unaware that Devlin Stone's Coalition had bypassed Halloran V due to newly constructed Drake SDS stations and Cockatrice Caspar II drones, the Capellan Confederation attacked on 11 February 3077 supported by upgraded Feng Huang-class cruiser CSS Franco Martell. Trapped in low orbit by activating Caspar II drones, Martell was destroyed with all hands on 27 February by a dozen surface-launched Killer Whale capital missiles.
