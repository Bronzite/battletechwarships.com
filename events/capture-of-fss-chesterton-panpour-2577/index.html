> ENGAGEMENT LOG // TAURIAN RAID ON PANPOUR (CAPTURE OF FSS CHESTERTON)

DATE: 2577 // SYSTEM: PANPOUR
[ ACTION LOG // TAURIAN RAID ON PANPOUR (CAPTURE OF FSS CHESTERTON) ]

Simultaneous with AFFS Admiral Sarah Vincent's assault on Tentativa, Taurian Marshal David Santos led half of his fleet in a surprise strike on the Federated Suns staging world of Panpour against Task Force 2.7. Among the outnumbered transports and carriers caught off-guard was FSS Chesterton while its entire aerospace group and much of its crew were off-ship.
