DATE: 2765-01-01 // SYSTEM: NEW-VANDENBURG
[ ACTION LOG // LOSS OF SLS NEBRASKA (PERIPHERY UPRISING) ]

During the 2765 Periphery Uprising, a Taurian-sabotaged fuel cargo ship drifted into the open cargo hold of the Texas-class battleship SLS Nebraska and detonated, splitting the Eleventh Fleet flagship in two. Wreckage struck the nearby battlecruiser SLS Cairo, badly damaging her before she limped clear.
