> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF SUDETEN & DESTRUCTION OF HAMMARR'S FALCON'S PERCH

DATE: 3136-04-03 to 3136-04-04 // SYSTEM: SUDETEN
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF SUDETEN & DESTRUCTION OF HAMMARR'S FALCON'S PERCH ]

On 3 April 3136, Mongol challenger Malvina Hazen jumped her Nightlord-class battleship CJF Emerald Talon and allied Clan Hell's Horses Congress-class frigate CHH Bucephalus to a pirate point 31 hours from Sudeten while Khan Jana Pryde's main Jade Falcon Naval Reserve armada (led by Whirlwind-class Jade Tornado) was more than four days outbound toward the Wolf border. Launching aboard her Aegis-class flagship CJF Jade Talon, Jana Pryde engaged Malvina's force in Sudeten orbit: Emerald Talon's broadsides crippled Jade Talon's main drives, while capital missiles and NPPCs punched into Emerald Talon's hangar deck and CIC, mortally wounding Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti. Malvina subsequently slew Jana Pryde in a BattleMech Trial of Possession upon Emerald Talon's hull and, on 4 April 3136, ordered dying Binetti to carry out 'Judgment' against Hammarr.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: Malvina Hazen claimed undisputed leadership of Clan Jade Falcon as Chingis Khan. After ordering Abandon Ship, fatally injured Star Admiral Dolphus Binetti piloted the 1.2-million-ton CJF Emerald Talon alone into atmospheric reentry on 4 April 3136; the unstreamlined battleship broke into three primary fragments and obliterated the Falcon's Perch tower, Loremaster Julia Buhalin, and central Hammarr.
