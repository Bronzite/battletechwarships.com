[ ACTION LOG // RAID ON BIKINI ATOLL (DESTRUCTION OF DCS AMBER LOTUS) ]

At 1430 hours on 18 December 3099, guided by Mimir intelligence, CGB Rasalhague jumped into Bikini Atoll transporting six Stars of Dominion Pocket WarShips and aerospace carriers. Inazuma-class corvette DCS Amber Lotus fought a desperate rearguard action with six fighters and three Okinawa-class DropShips to cover the flight of stripped factory WarShip hulks Yamato and Ryū.
