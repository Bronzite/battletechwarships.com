> ENGAGEMENT LOG // RAID ON BIKINI ATOLL (DESTRUCTION OF DCS AMBER LOTUS, YAMATO & RYŪ)

DATE: 3099-12-18 // SYSTEM: BIKINI-ATOLL
[ ACTION LOG // RAID ON BIKINI ATOLL (DESTRUCTION OF DCS AMBER LOTUS, YAMATO & RYŪ) ]

At 1430 hours on 18 December 3099, guided by Mimir intelligence, CGB Rasalhague jumped into Bikini Atoll one-tenth of a light-second from the Combine mobile industrial anchorage with six Stars of Dominion Vanir-class Pocket WarShips and fighter-carrier DropShips. While stripped factory WarShips DCS Yamato and DCS Ryū burned in opposite directions, Inazuma-class corvette DCS Amber Lotus under Sho-sho Emanuel Inoue launched six fighters and undocked Okinawa-class DropShips Heated Sword, Iijima's Pride, and Comforting Silence in a desperate rearguard stand.
