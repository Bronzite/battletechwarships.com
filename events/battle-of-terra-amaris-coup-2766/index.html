> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF TERRA (OPERATION APOTHEOSIS OUTBREAK)

DATE: 2766-12-27 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF TERRA (OPERATION APOTHEOSIS OUTBREAK) ]

On 27 December 2766, three hundred Rim Worlds Republic WarShips erupted over Terra in support of Stefan Amaris's coup while Terra's Space Defense System command centers were neutralized. Star Squadron (led by First Lord flagship SLS Star League) and the High Command group (led by SLS Enterprise, SLS Theodor Logan, and SLS Kharkov) fought an eighteen-hour running battle from Unity City to Geneva against overwhelming numbers.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: SLS Enterprise and escorting ships shattered RWS Stefan Amaris over the Pacific, and SLA survivors purged Republican WarShips from orbit over the next day, but Star League, Enterprise, and on 29 December at 2127 TST SLS Theodor Logan were all destroyed, while SLS Kharkov escaped carrying High Command evacuees from Mount Baker.
