> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BOARDING AND CAPTURE OF DCS URIZEN II AT COURCHEVEL

DATE: 3063-08-15 // SYSTEM: COURCHEVEL
[ ACTION LOG // BOARDING AND CAPTURE OF DCS URIZEN II AT COURCHEVEL ]

Emerging at Courchevel's nadir jump point aboard JumpShip November Wind and DropShip Burning Paw on 15 August 3063, Star Captain Jake Kabrinski's disgraced Gamma Trinary spotted the Draconis Combine Kirishima-class heavy cruiser DCS Urizen II poised to strike Ghost Bear holdings. Lacking boarding shuttles, Kabrinski launched forty-nine EVA-rigged Elementals across open vacuum from November Wind, endured heavy anti-fighter point-defense fire losing twenty-six warriors, breached an aft bridge pressure hatch with demolition charges and support lasers, and stormed the bridge and engine room.
