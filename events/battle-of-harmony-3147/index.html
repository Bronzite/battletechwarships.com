> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF HARMONY (AMBUSH OF CSF SILENTSHARK)

DATE: 3147-01-31 to 3147-01-31 // SYSTEM: HARMONY
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF HARMONY (AMBUSH OF CSF SILENTSHARK) ]

During Clan Sea Fox's 3147 economic blockade of the Regulan Fiefs, roughly two dozen Regulan Leopard- and Union-class assault DropShips ambushed Spina Khanate Alpha Aimag flagship CSF Silentshark at Harmony's zenith jump point with nuclear-tipped capital missiles while attached NL-45 gunboat Stalker of the Deep was overseeing a JumpShip inspection. Multiple nuclear strikes damaged Silentshark's maneuvering thrusters and tore deep craters in her flank before Hammerhead Star's Nagasawa-class DropShips (including Fifth Estate and the lost Sphyrnidae) and Stalker of the Deep annihilated the entire attacking Regulan force.
