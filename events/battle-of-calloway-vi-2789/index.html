> ENGAGEMENT LOG // DEBACLE AT CALLOWAY VI

DATE: 2789 // SYSTEM: CALLOWAY-VI
[ ACTION LOG // DEBACLE AT CALLOWAY VI ]

In March 2789, Colonel Devlin led 30 WarShips—including flagship CCS Sundermann Liao and destroyer CCS Calseraigne—in Chancellor Barbara Liao's personal retaliatory raid on Calloway VI. Betrayed in advance to the League, the task force's unguarded JumpShips at zenith were annihilated by a Marik relief fleet including FWLS Pleiades, forcing Devlin to sacrifice a rear guard to cover the Chancellor's escape.
