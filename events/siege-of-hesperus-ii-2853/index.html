> ENGAGEMENT LOG // RELIEF OF HESPERUS II (2853)

DATE: 2853-10-25 // SYSTEM: HESPERUS-II
[ ACTION LOG // RELIEF OF HESPERUS II (2853) ]

To break a six-warship Draconis Combine blockade isolating Hesperus II in autumn 2853, Triumvirate member Henry DeCalidore reactivated the mothballed Tharkad-class battlecruiser LCS Invincible with retired naval veterans and academy crews. On 25 October 2853, LCS Invincible and her relief fleet destroyed the Combine blockade and provided orbit-to-surface fire support for the Force of Last Resort.
