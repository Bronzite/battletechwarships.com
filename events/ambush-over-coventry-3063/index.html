> ENGAGEMENT LOG // DESTRUCTION OF LAS ARTHUR STEINER-DAVION OVER COVENTRY

DATE: 3063-10-09 // SYSTEM: COVENTRY
[ ACTION LOG // DESTRUCTION OF LAS ARTHUR STEINER-DAVION OVER COVENTRY ]

Cruising through Coventry's umbra on 9 October 3063 with three ship-killer Overlord DropShips, Admiral Jurgen Haas deployed the newly renamed Fox-class corvette LAS Arthur Steiner-Davion (ex-Angela Franks) to ambush Prince Victor Steiner-Davion's expected rebel cruiser Melissa Davion. Instead, sensors detected an anomalous over-eight-minute IR emergence signature at a pirate point 170 kilometers off his bow as the Word of Blake Aegis-class heavy cruiser WoBS Immortal Spirit executed an experimental long-range WarShip superjump into Coventry.

However, the superjump blew out Immortal Spirit's Kearny-Fuchida drive, stranding her in Coventry's outer asteroid belt and gas-giant ice rings until 3074 and leaving the Lyran Alliance to list Arthur Steiner-Davion as Missing in Action.
