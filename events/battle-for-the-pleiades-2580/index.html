> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE FOR THE PLEIADES

DATE: 2580-02-11 // SYSTEM: MAIA
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE FOR THE PLEIADES ]

Launched on 11 February 2580 by SLDF Second Battle Fleet reinforced by First Battle Fleet against the Taurian Pleiades Fleet and explosive fireships hidden among the Deep Pleiades asteroids. Over three days of brutal close-quarters combat, fifteen Star League and thirteen Taurian WarShips were destroyed before surviving Concordat vessels slipped out at proximity points.
