[ ACTION LOG // TAMAR TIGERS RAID ON LUTHIEN ]

In March 2787, Colonel (Duke) Graham Kelswa's Tamar Tigers arrived via pirate point off the Draconis Combine capital escorted by the Potemkin-class cruiser LCS Nightwind and 25 attack/carrier DropShips, confronting a solitary Naga-class destroyer (DCS Blue Lotus) and an unfinished Narukami II hull in drydock.
