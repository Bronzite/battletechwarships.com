> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF DIERON (NEBULOS JUNKYARD ACTION)

DATE: 2787 // SYSTEM: DIERON
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF DIERON (NEBULOS JUNKYARD ACTION) ]

In April 2787, a Lyran Commonwealth task force built around Tharkad-class battlecruiser LCS Coventry, Commonwealth II cruisers Gallery and York, Aegis cruiser Ironwood, and Mako corvettes Anthemius and Heraclius raided Dieron's SLDF shipyards, engaging six defending Combine WarShips sheltering in the radioactive Junkyard debris around Nebulos.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: LCS Coventry's battle group sank all six Combine WarShips (Selene, Radstadt, Akiko, Maja, Jarett, Sawyer) and battered the Dieron yards while losing Anthemius and Heraclius; yet while Coventry, Gallery, York, and Ironwood rendezvoused behind Bemer/Nebulos to evacuate Coventry's crew, all four vanished mysteriously without trace.
