[ ACTION LOG // STEEL VIPER ASSAULT & WITHDRAWAL FROM LUM ]

On 8 September 3065, Clan Steel Viper WarShips punched through a weakened Snow Raven cordon to land troops on industrial capital Lum. When Clan Jade Falcon threatened Trials against Viper holdings, Khan Perigard Zalman ordered an un-hegiraed withdrawal on 12 October.
