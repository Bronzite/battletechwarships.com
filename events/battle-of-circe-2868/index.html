DATE: 2868 // SYSTEM: CIRCE
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF CIRCE (TRIAL OF ABSORPTION OF CLAN MONGOOSE) ]

During the 2868 Trial of Absorption of Clan Mongoose declared by Smoke Jaguar Khan Theodore Osis, nearly thirty WarShips alongside dozens of DropShips and scores of aerospace fighters clashed above Circe in the largest fleet battle in Clan history. Outmatched in heavier Smoke Jaguar hulls, Mongoose Star Colonel Jenna Mercedes's thirty Chippewa IIC aerospace fighters shredded the aft thrusters of the McKenna-class flagship CSJ Obsidian before Du Shi Wang-class battleship CMS Quicksilver Mongoose raked Obsidian with twelve bow NPPCs and shattered her spine. Lola III-class destroyer CSJ Storm Cat then crippled Quicksilver Mongoose, turning the battle's momentum as Mongoose fighters expended themselves over Circe.
