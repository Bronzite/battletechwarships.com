> ENGAGEMENT LOG // SPACE BATTLE OVER WAZAN

DATE: 2787 // SYSTEM: WAZAN
[ ACTION LOG // SPACE BATTLE OVER WAZAN ]

Executed in tandem with the July 2787 Capellan counterattack on Corey, a Confederation squadron of two Essex and two Lola III destroyers led by the Black Lion-class battlecruiser CCS Zulfiqar struck Wazan's parked League transports and engaged the responding Marik battleship Ragusa and Lola destroyers Minsck and Warsaw.
