> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF SKONDIA

DATE: 2787-02 // SYSTEM: SKONDIA
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF SKONDIA ]

Two months after Coordinator Minoru Kurita's declaration of war, Tai-sho Hiroshi Pederson arrived at Skondia's nadir jump station with forty-five Draconis Combine WarShips, twenty JumpShips, and assault DropShips carrying six DCMS 'Mech regiments, expecting to spearhead a feint toward Skye. Instead, he encountered Admiral Seamus van Hatten's emergency Lyran concentration—nearly three hundred combat ships centered on fifty WarShips, including the Tharkad-class battlecruisers LCS Impregnable and LCS Indomitable, rushed together after Free Worlds raids hit Commonwealth shipyards.
