> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF TENTATIVA (CASE AMBER)

DATE: 2577-09-28 // SYSTEM: TENTATIVA
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF TENTATIVA (CASE AMBER) ]

In September-October 2577, Taurian Marshal David Santos baited AFFS Admiral Sarah Vincent's Task Force Four into an elaborate multi-system trap at Tentativa while simultaneously striking Panpour. Split between Tentativa's jump points, outnumbered Davion WarShips including FSS Sword's Point and FSS Eagle's Eye were caught by converged Taurian detachments.
