> ENGAGEMENT LOG // ORBITAL DUEL OVER TIKONOV

DATE: 3065-02-21 // SYSTEM: TIKONOV
[ ACTION LOG // ORBITAL DUEL OVER TIKONOV ]

As Prince Victor Steiner-Davion prepared to withdraw his Allied brigades from Tikonov on 21 February 3065, the Loyalist Fox-class corvette LAS Katrina Steiner closed into low orbit to bombard Allied surface positions and interdict the departing DropShips. The Allied Avalon-class flagship FCS Melissa Davion intercepted the corvette in orbital space.
