> ENGAGEMENT LOG // SYRMA AMBUSH

DATE: 2338-07-23 // SYSTEM: SYRMA
[ ACTION LOG // SYRMA AMBUSH ]

On 23 July 2338, Vice-Admiral Konrad McKenna led a Terran Hegemony invasion armada in two blind consecutive waves into Syrma, unaware that the Federation of Skye had seeded the jump points with mines and dormant Sleeper missiles. Twenty-seven of 29 troopships and 34 Hegemony warships were lost before Director-General James McKenna arrived aboard THS Black Lion to relieve his son and escort survivors back to Terra.
