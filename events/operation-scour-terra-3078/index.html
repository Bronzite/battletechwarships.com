> ENGAGEMENT LOG // OPERATION SCOUR: COALITION NAVAL ASSAULT ON TERRA & TITAN YARDS

DATE: 3078-08-15 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION SCOUR: COALITION NAVAL ASSAULT ON TERRA & TITAN YARDS ]

Beginning at 08:00 Zulu on 15 August 3078, Devlin Stone's Coalition launched the decisive two-pronged naval offensive against the Word of Blake heartland. Admiral Alex Douglas's Task Force Cronus—anchored by Snow Raven carrier CSR Corvidae, Falcon battlecruiser CJF Jade Aerie, and cruiser CJF Blue Talon—moved to neutralize Titan Yards, while Precentor-Admiral Alain Beresick's Task Force Earthbound—led by CSV Invisible Truth alongside Lyran, Combine, FedSuns, Nova Cat, Diamond Shark, and Ghost Bear warships—shattered orbital defenses around Terra to open landing zones.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: Despite catastrophic Coalition naval casualties—including the destruction of NCS Future Triumph over Terra and CSR Corvidae during the Titan campaign—Stone's Coalition secured orbital supremacy over Terra, ending the Word of Blake Protectorate. Surviving Nova Cat WarShips NCS Blade and NCS Hunter later entered Republic of the Sphere service as RSS Auspicium and RSS Triumphus.
