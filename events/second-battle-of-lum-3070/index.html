> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLES OF LUM (3070 VIPER CONQUEST & RAVEN COUNTERSTRIKE)

DATE: 3070-09-25 // SYSTEM: LUM
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLES OF LUM (3070 VIPER CONQUEST & RAVEN COUNTERSTRIKE) ]

Fresh from savaging Diamond Shark ships over Strana Mechty, saKhan Hoskins led CSV Perigard Zalman and the Steel Viper fleet against Clan Snow Raven at Lum, conquering the system after a seven-hour battle. Five days later, the surviving Snow Raven fleet launched a ferocious ambush on the repairing Zalman, including a failed ramming attack by CSR Black Justice.
