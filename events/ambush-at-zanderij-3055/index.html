DATE: 3055-08-20 // SYSTEM: ZANDERIJ
[ ACTION LOG // PIRATE-POINT AMBUSH AT ZANDERIJ ]

Anticipating the Red Corsair's movements after the battle of Yeguas, Caitlin Kell led combined Kell Hound aerospace wings and a Star of Thirty-first Wolf Solahma fighters in a dual ambush among Zanderij's asteroid fields. When the Congress-class frigate Fire Rose arrived and deployed two Overlord DropShips on 20 August 3055, the ambushers destroyed one Overlord before Fire Rose's laser fire covered the survivor's escape.
