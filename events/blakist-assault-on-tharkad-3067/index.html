[ ACTION LOG // WORD OF BLAKE NUCLEAR STRIKE ON THARKAD ]

Moments after the Second Star League formally dissolved at the Tharkad Conference on 5 December 3067, Word of Blake fleets launched simultaneous assaults on Tharkad and New Avalon.
