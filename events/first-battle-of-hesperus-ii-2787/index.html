> ENGAGEMENT LOG // FIRST BATTLE OF HESPERUS II (OPERATION BROKEN BLADE)

DATE: 2787-12-01 // SYSTEM: HESPERUS-II
[ ACTION LOG // FIRST BATTLE OF HESPERUS II (OPERATION BROKEN BLADE) ]

In December 2787, Draconis Combine WarShips and four 'Mech regiments launched Operation BROKEN BLADE to cripple Defiance Industries in retaliation for the Tamar Tigers' raid on Luthien. Admiral Luther Weisskopf's Commonwealth defense fleet blocked the Combine fleet from bombarding the underground Defiance factories, though Tai-sho Pederson melted the orbiting shipyards before withdrawing in January 2788.
