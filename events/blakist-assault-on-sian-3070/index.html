DATE: 3070 // SYSTEM: SIAN
[ ACTION LOG // WORD OF BLAKE INVASION OF SIAN ]

In late 3070, while Capellan citizens celebrated the return of troops from the Hasek campaign, three Word of Blake Militia divisions escorted by two WarShips launched a surprise invasion of Sian; newly modified Feng Huang-class heavy cruisers CCS Ilsa Hyung and CCS Aleisha Kris scrambled with two Impavido destroyers to meet them.
