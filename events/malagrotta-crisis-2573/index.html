DATE: 2573-02-19 // SYSTEM: MALAGROTTA
[ ACTION LOG // THE MALAGROTTA CRISIS (FONTANA CLASH) ]

After navigational and communications failures caused five Taurian WarShips (including a Winchester cruiser, two Wagon Wheel frigates, and a Pinto corvette) to stray from Drexa into the demilitarized Fontana venture, panicking Davion officials ordered Federated Suns forces to open fire on 19 February 2573.
