DATE: 3146-08-13 // SYSTEM: MARLETTE
[ ACTION LOG // THE MARLETTE DECEPTION & LOSS OF CCS ILSA HYUNG ]

Lured by Erik Sandoval's MIIO misinformation into targeting an allegedly under-defended logistics depot, a Capellan task force built around Second McCarron's Armored Cavalry and Feng Huang-class cruiser CCS Ilsa Hyung emerged at a Marlette pirate point on 13 August 3146. Although Ilsa Hyung broke the Fifth Crucis Lancers by orbital fire, AFFS Task Force Panoply and Clan Sea Fox Gamma Aimag ambushed the cruiser in orbit and broke her keel.
