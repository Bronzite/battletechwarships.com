DATE: 3072-08-13 // SYSTEM: MARKESAN
[ ACTION LOG // NAVAL ACTION OVER MARKESAN (L1 LAGRANGE POINT) ]

On 13 August 3072, a Word of Blake task force consisting of an unidentified WarShip, two capital-missile Pocket WarShips, and troop transports attempted an assault at Markesan's L1 LaGrange point. Aerospace fighters of the Fourth Crucis Lancers swiftly eliminated both Pocket WarShips while hidden AFFS Fox-class corvettes FSS Admiral Michael Saille and FSS Brest jumped in-system to defeat and board the escort WarShip.
