DATE: 3068-03-09 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION CASE WHITE (COMSTAR ASSAULT ON TERRA) ]

On 9 March 3068, ComStar launched CASE WHITE—the largest fleet engagement of the century—into the teeth of Terra's Word of Blake WarShips and Caspar II drone squadrons, undermined by Blakist sleeper sabotage on Com Guard vessels.
