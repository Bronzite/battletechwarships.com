DATE: 2806-01-07 // SYSTEM: ORIENTE
[ ACTION LOG // RELIEF OF ORIENTE (THIRD CAPELLAN SIEGE) ]

Responding to House Liao's third invasion of Oriente, a Free Worlds League relief fleet centered on Atreus-class battleship FWLS Bismarck and Soyal-class cruisers FWLS Karka and FWLS Makara destroyed Capellan transport JumpShips at zenith, recharged, and executed an ambush jump to Oriente's lunar Lagrange point against CCS Duncan Liao, four Vincent corvettes, and two Essex destroyers.
