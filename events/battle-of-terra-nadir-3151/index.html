> ENGAGEMENT LOG // OPERATION NOSTOS: BATTLE OF TERRA (NADIR JUMP POINT)

DATE: 3151-01-01 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION NOSTOS: BATTLE OF TERRA (NADIR JUMP POINT) ]

At 0711 GMT on 1 January 3151 (T-Minus 9), Clan Wolf's Task Force Mercury jumped to Terra's nadir jump point spearheaded by CWS Jerome Winson and Khan Alaric Ward's recovered flagship SLS McKenna's Pride. Star Admiral Haake Sukhanov formed six Wolf WarShips—SLS McKenna's Pride, CWS Dire Wolf, CWS Bloody Fang, CWS Rogue, CWS Victoria Ward, and CWS Jerome Winson—in a tight defensive shell around 277 troop transports while Jian-class cutters launched Ebony Wolves Elementals onto Republic hulls. Admiral Jean-Jacques Labbe's Task Force Hannibal (RSS Abundantia, RSS Auspicium, RSS Triumphus, RSS Shield of the Republic, and Gladius Terrae) charged the Wolf line, triggering a fierce capital-ship melee.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME: RSS Triumphus had its keel broken by combined fire from SLS McKenna's Pride and CWS Dire Wolf; RSS Auspicium suffered catastrophic bow damage colliding with Wolf assault DropShip CWS White Lupu during a desperate ramming bid; and RSS Shield of the Republic was crippled by Ebony Wolves boarders. Forced to preserve his surviving ships, Admiral Labbe withdrew RSS Abundantia and towed Shield of the Republic toward Fort Grec, leaving Terra's nadir approach open to Clan Wolf.
