> ENGAGEMENT LOG // FORTRESS REPUBLIC WALL BOUNCE-BACK OF DCS PRIDE OF KURITA

DATE: 3137-01-01 // SYSTEM: DIERON
[ ACTION LOG // FORTRESS REPUBLIC WALL BOUNCE-BACK OF DCS PRIDE OF KURITA ]

In late December 3136, Draconis Combine heir Tai-sho Theodore Kurita staged aboard the Kyushu-class frigate DCS Pride of Kurita at Deneb Algedi to spearhead a synchronized New Year's Eve assault into Republic-held Altair. Striking the Fortress Republic Wall barrier, the WarShip suffered a catastrophic hyperspace bounce-back and was catapulted to Dieron's nadir jump point at 0005 hours on 1 January 3137 as a corkscrew-twisted, hydrogen-depleted derelict.
