DATE: 3149-07-09 // SYSTEM: DUBHE
[ ACTION LOG // ORBITAL BATTLE AND SCUTTLING OF RSS FLATUS OVER DUBHE ]

When a Clan Wolf armada jumped to Dubhe's zenith point on 9 July 3149 to crush Damien Redburn's Republic Remnant, the Fidelis-crewed Dante-class frigate RSS Flatus (formerly ComStar CSS Bordeaux) intercepted a Binary of Fourth Wolf Guard Assault Cluster DropShips burning for orbital insertion. Flatus destroyed two DropShips by broadside and shot down several more after her K-F drive was crippled before losing weapons power and fighting a fierce deck-by-deck boarding defense.
