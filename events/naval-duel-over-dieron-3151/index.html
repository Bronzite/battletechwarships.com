> ENGAGEMENT LOG // NAVAL DUEL OVER DIERON (ALEISHA KRIS VS DRACONIS RIFT)

DATE: 3151-10-20 // SYSTEM: DIERON
[ ACTION LOG // NAVAL DUEL OVER DIERON (ALEISHA KRIS VS DRACONIS RIFT) ]

Covering the Fifth MAC and Warrior House Ijori assault on Dieron on 20 October 3151, Capellan Feng Huang-class cruiser CCS Aleisha Kris (Col. Jamsaxa Cutler) disabled primary fire controls on DCS Draconis Rift (Capt. Sariah Kaur) at the zenith jump point before a second matched-orbit duel eight days later enabled Capellan DropShips to scatter and reach the surface.
