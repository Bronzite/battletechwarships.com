> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF MONTOUR

DATE: 2587-10-11 // SYSTEM: MONTOUR
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF MONTOUR ]

Intercepting Marshal David Santos' Taurian relief expedition and Eighth Provisional Corps at Montour, Admiral Yukiko Pequeno's SLDF Fourth Fleet and Admiral Helden van der Geest's Federated Suns squadron fought a running five-day battle around the system's outer planets and asteroid belts.
