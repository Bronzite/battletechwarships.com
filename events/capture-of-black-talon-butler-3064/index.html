> ENGAGEMENT LOG // DRYDOCK RAID & CAPTURE OF CJF BLACK TALON AT BUTLER

DATE: 3064-10-17 // SYSTEM: BUTLER
[ ACTION LOG // DRYDOCK RAID & CAPTURE OF CJF BLACK TALON AT BUTLER ]

During General Archer Christifori's 17 October 3064 strike on Butler, DropShip Colonel Crockett Captain Lee Fullerton deduced from cold fusion sensors that the Jade Falcon Aegis-class cruiser CJF Black Talon had her reactors offline in drydock. Although Star Admiral Martin Thastus cut moorings on jump batteries alone and challenged the raiders, concentrated fighter strikes toward her bow breached the CIC and compelled Thastus to surrender the cruiser intact as prize for Clan Wolf-in-Exile.
