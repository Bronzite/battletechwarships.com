> ENGAGEMENT LOG // CLASH OF THE TITAN SHIPYARDS

DATE: 3151-01-02 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // CLASH OF THE TITAN SHIPYARDS ]

Following Clan Wolf's breakout at Terra's nadir jump point, Star Admiral Haake Sukhanov dispatched Task Force Hyperion—centered on the Cameron-class battlecruiser CWS Bloody Fang, Isegrim-class escort DropShips, Jian-class Ebony Wolves cutters, and fighter wings—to seize the Titan Shipyards and deny Task Force Hannibal repair docks. Unbeknownst to Wolf intelligence, Warwick Station and two accompanying Bastion-class stations had been refitted with capital-scale weaponry, crippling Bloody Fang with power loss and a blown helium seal. Sukhanov diverted the Liberator-class cruiser CWS Victoria Ward to save Hyperion, enduring a massive aerofighter furball and a hull-penetrating capital-missile salvo before Wolf Isegrim escorts concentrated fire to destroy Warwick Station.
