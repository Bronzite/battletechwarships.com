> ENGAGEMENT LOG // ORBITAL BATTLE OF LUZERNE (OPERATION BIRDDOG)

DATE: 3059-06-29 // SYSTEM: LUZERNE
[ ACTION LOG // ORBITAL BATTLE OF LUZERNE (OPERATION BIRDDOG) ]

On 29 June 3059, the Com Guard Sovetskii Soyuz-class heavy cruiser SLS Harmonious Thought escorted the Operation BIRDDOG invasion flotilla into Luzerne only to confront two unexpected Clan Smoke Jaguar WarShips. Both SLS Harmonious Thought and the Jaguar destroyer CSJ Storm Cat were destroyed, CSJ Snow Leopard was crippled, and half the allied troop transports were lost before ground forces fought a grueling six-week liberation campaign.
