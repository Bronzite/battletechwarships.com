> ENGAGEMENT LOG // SKIRMISH AT CINNABAR (WRECK OF FSS KALI)

DATE: 2940-11-03 // SYSTEM: CINNABAR
[ ACTION LOG // SKIRMISH AT CINNABAR (WRECK OF FSS KALI) ]

After Lyran Ghost Company and science teams located the century-old wreck of Soyal-class battleship FSS Kali on Cinnabar on 22 October 2940, accidentally powering its HPG alerted the Draconis Combine Vigilant-class corvette DCS Kuromaru. From 3 to 5 November 2940, the grounded Kali fired jury-rigged lasers and a Gauss cannon to cripple a Combine DropShip before DCS Kuromaru bombarded the wreck and landed two 'Mech companies. Salvagers detonated Kali's datacore prior to LCS Wagner's arrival on 6 November.
