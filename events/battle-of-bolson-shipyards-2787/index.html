[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF BOLSON SHIPYARDS (KYOTO) ]

A Free Worlds League naval attack group centered on Cameron-class battlecruiser FWLS Rasalas engaged Hauptmann-Kommodore Ustus Tillbert's five Lyran corvette squadrons guarding the Bolson Shipyards over Kyoto from 14 to 17 February 2787.
