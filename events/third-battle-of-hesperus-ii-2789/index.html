> ENGAGEMENT LOG // THIRD BATTLE OF HESPERUS II

DATE: 2789-03-01 // SYSTEM: HESPERUS-II
[ ACTION LOG // THIRD BATTLE OF HESPERUS II ]

Admiral Thaddeus Marik jumped a Free Worlds League fleet into Hesperus' inward solar jump point to strike Defiance Industries by surprise, only to be intercepted when Admiral Oscar F. Dewey's LCS Granite and LCS Endeavor performed emergency in-system jumps to Hesperus II's lunar LaGrange point.
