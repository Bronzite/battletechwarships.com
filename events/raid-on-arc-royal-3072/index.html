> ENGAGEMENT LOG // WORD OF BLAKE RAID ON ARC-ROYAL

DATE: 3072-03-22 // SYSTEM: ARC-ROYAL
[ ACTION LOG // WORD OF BLAKE RAID ON ARC-ROYAL ]

On 22 March 3072, Word of Blake forces dispatched the Second, Thirty-eighth, and Forty-third Shadow Divisions escorted by FWL-origin WarShips Aeneas, Troy, and Olympic plus nuclear-armed Pocket WarShips to raid Arc-Royal. Clan Wolf-in-Exile's Wolf Pack Naval Reserve intercepted the invaders in deep system space.
