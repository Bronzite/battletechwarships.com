[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF COLLEEN (ANNIHILATION OF CLAN BLOOD SPIRIT) ]

Following the discovery of Clan Blood Spirit's hidden refuge system of Colleen, Clan Star Adder arrived at the zenith jump point on 5 October 3084. Led by the McKenna-class battleship CSA Sovereign Right and the Nightlord-class battleship CSA Absolute Truth, the Adders overwhelmed the Spirits' final naval defenders—Carrack-class CBS Kari-anna Schmitt and York-class CBS Stooping Kite—before landing three Galaxies on Haven and using Absolute Truth to level orbital and planetary Spirit enclaves.
