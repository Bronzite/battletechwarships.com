> ENGAGEMENT LOG // CAPELLAN COUNTER-BLOCKADE & SPACE BATTLE OF COREY

DATE: 2787 // SYSTEM: COREY
[ ACTION LOG // CAPELLAN COUNTER-BLOCKADE & SPACE BATTLE OF COREY ]

Following the July 2787 Free Worlds League landings on Corey, a superior Capellan Confederation battle group led by the Congress-class frigate CCS Tianjin with four Essex destroyers and two Vincent corvettes overwhelmed Marik transports and drew FWLS Agrippa, Oran, Ocuri, and Samhain into an in-system jump battle.
