> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF THARKAD (WOLF-IN-EXILE & LYRAN ALLIANCE ENGAGEMENT)

DATE: 3066-08-24 // SYSTEM: THARKAD
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF THARKAD (WOLF-IN-EXILE & LYRAN ALLIANCE ENGAGEMENT) ]

Escorting roughly fifty Allied DropShips carrying the First Kell Hounds, Blue Star Irregulars, and Twentieth Arcturan Guards RCT in support of Peter Steiner-Davion, Khan Phelan Kell's McKenna-class Werewolf—supported by an unnamed Wolf-in-Exile Cameron-class battlecruiser, Black Lion-class battlecruiser, and the Blue Star Irregulars' captured Fredasa-class raider Kerensky's Blues—clashed with Lyran Alliance defenders led by the new Mjolnir-class battlecruiser LAS Yggdrasil and a Fox-class corvette.
