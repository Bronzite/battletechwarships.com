> ENGAGEMENT LOG // OPERATION LIBERATION: TERRAN LANDINGS & MARS DRONE ACTION

DATE: 2777-01-30 // SYSTEM: TERRA
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION LIBERATION: TERRAN LANDINGS & MARS DRONE ACTION ]

Beginning on 30 January 2777, General Aleksandr Kerensky spearheaded Operation LIBERATION aboard SLS McKenna's Pride to break Stefan Amaris's Space Defense System and land thirty-two divisions across thirteen Eurasian landing zones, including direct WarShip fire against the Kumbu Himalayan SDS array. Simultaneously along the Mars approaches, SLS California, SLS Red Bluff, and SLS Fred T. Berry intercepted six Rim Worlds Caspar M-7 automated drone WarShips to shield twelve troop-laden DropShips.
