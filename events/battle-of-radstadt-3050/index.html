> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF RADSTADT (DEATH OF ILKHAN LEO SHOWERS)

DATE: 3050-10-31 // SYSTEM: RADSTADT
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF RADSTADT (DEATH OF ILKHAN LEO SHOWERS) ]

On 31 October 3050, fleeing Clan Wolf's conquest of Rasalhague with only the First Drakon aerospace wing for escort, Elected Prince Haakon Magnusson arrived at Radstadt directly under the guns of Clan WarShips. To buy time for Magnusson's JumpShip to quick-charge and escape, Captain Tyra Miraborg led the First Drakon into battle and rammed her crippled Shilone aerospace fighter straight into the bridge of Clan Wolf's flagship CWS Dire Wolf, killing ilKhan Leo Showers instantly.
