> ENGAGEMENT LOG // BATTLE OF FLINTOFT

DATE: 2581-03-15 // SYSTEM: FLINTOFT
[ ACTION LOG // BATTLE OF FLINTOFT ]

On 15 March 2581, the SLDF First Battle Fleet jumped into Flintoft and caught a delayed Taurian fleet arming local defense flotillas with explosive fireships. Over a nine-hour battle, sixteen Star League WarShips were crippled or destroyed in exchange for nine Taurian WarShips, over twenty DropShips, and more than a hundred fireships.
