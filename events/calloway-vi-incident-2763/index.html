> ENGAGEMENT LOG // CALLOWAY VI PATROL CLASH

DATE: 2763 // SYSTEM: CALLOWAY-VI
[ ACTION LOG // CALLOWAY VI PATROL CLASH ]

In 2763 a flotilla from the SLDF Seventh Fleet jumped into the Calloway VI system, where the Second Free Worlds League Navy Fleet was conducting patrol maneuvers. Conflicting identification or nerves ignited an unplanned exchange of capital-ship fire—resulting in the immediate destruction of the League-class destroyer FWLS Albert Marik and heavy damage to both FWLS Olympic and SLDF vessel SLS Hartford before commanders restored an immediate ceasefire.
