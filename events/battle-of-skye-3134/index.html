> ENGAGEMENT LOG // OPERATION LODESTONE: BATTLE OF SKYE

DATE: 3134-12-09 // SYSTEM: SKYE
[ ACTION LOG // OPERATION LODESTONE: BATTLE OF SKYE ]

On 9 December 3134, executing a daring Lagrange-point shell game that swapped out an unarmored JumpShip for House Steiner's sole surviving Mjolnir-class battlecruiser, Kaptain Lionel Brionns and honorary Kommodore Eduard Goran brought LCS Yggdrasil and four assault DropShips into Skye directly against the blockading Clan Jade Falcon battleship Emerald Talon. After bridge hits injured Kaptain Brionns and severed comms to Central Control, Goran assumed command, rolled the battered battlecruiser to shield its wounded port side, and feigned a point-blank collision that forced Emerald Talon to thrust down and expose its ventral aft.
