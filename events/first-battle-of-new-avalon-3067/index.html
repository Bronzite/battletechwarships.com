DATE: 3067-12-05 to 3067-12-31 // SYSTEM: NEW-AVALON
[ ACTION LOG // FIRST BATTLE OF NEW AVALON (JIHAD INVASION) ]

On 5 December 3067, three lurking Word of Blake WarShips—WBS Divine Forgiveness, WBS Red Angel, and WBS Herald of Justice—jumped into New Avalon to initiate the Jihad assault. Defending Fox-class corvettes FSS Bryceland and FCS Antrim mounted a hopeless countercharge, destroying themselves in a suicide collision that crippled WBS Herald of Justice.
